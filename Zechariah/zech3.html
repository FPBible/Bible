Case File: Divine Council Proceedings // Night Vision 05

Zechariah 4

The Council's Supply: The Two Anointed Ones and the Leveling of the Mountain

1. Judicial Context: The Sod (Divine Council)

Zechariah 4 provides the pneumatological (Spirit-driven) foundation for the legal transitions decreed in Chapter 3. The fifth night vision focuses on the executive authority of the **Divine Council** to supply the New Creation Temple. The central conflict is the "Great Mountain"—the established, apostate Old Covenant system that stood in opposition to the New House. The decree is issued that this Mountain will be legally leveled "not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit." Furthermore, the vision introduces the "Two Anointed Ones" who stand by the Lord of the whole earth. This is the immediate fulfillment of the promise made in Zechariah 3:7—human representatives have now been granted "access" into the celestial Council to mediate the golden oil of the Spirit to the lampstand.

2. Scripture (WEB Technical)

1 The angel who talked with me came again and wakened me, as a man who is wakened out of his sleep. 2 He said to me, "What do you see?" I said, "I have seen, and behold, a lampstand all of gold, with its bowl on the top of it, and its seven lamps on it; there are seven pipes to each of the lamps, which are on the top of it; 3 and two olive trees by it, one on the right side of the bowl, and the other on the left side of it."
4 I answered and spoke to the angel who talked with me, saying, "What are these, my lord?" 5 Then the angel who talked with me answered me, "Don't you know what these are?" I said, "No, my lord."
6 Then he answered and spoke to me, saying, "This is the word of Yahweh to Zerubbabel, saying, '**Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit**,' says Yahweh of Armies. 7 **Who are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you are a plain**; and he will bring out the capstone with shouts of 'Grace, grace, to it!'"
8 Moreover the word of Yahweh came to me, saying, 9 "The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house. His hands shall also finish it; and you will know that Yahweh of Armies has sent me to you. 10 For who has despised the day of small things? For these seven shall rejoice, and shall see the plummet in the hand of Zerubbabel. These are the eyes of Yahweh, which run back and forth through the whole earth."
11 Then I answered and asked him, "What are these two olive trees on the right side of the lampstand and on the left side of it?" 12 I asked him a second time, "What are these two olive branches, which are beside the two golden spouts, that pour the golden oil out of themselves?" 13 He answered me, "Don't you know what these are?" I said, "No, my lord." 14 Then he said, "**These are the two anointed ones who stand by the Lord of the whole earth.**"

3. Fulfillment Notes: Executive Supply

  • The Great Mountain Leveled (v. 7): In apocalyptic language, a mountain signifies a ruling kingdom or religious administration. Jesus commanded "this mountain" (the Old Covenant Temple system) to be cast into the sea (Matt 21:21). This judicial leveling was executed in AD 70.
  • The Capstone (v. 7): The final stone that completes the spiritual Temple, fulfilled in Christ (Eph 2:20). Its presentation with shouts of "Grace" marks the legal end of the works-based administration.
  • Not by Might or Power (v. 6): The transition from the Old Covenant (which relied on human kings, armies, and physical priesthoods) to the New Covenant, which is administered entirely by the Holy Spirit from the Heavenly Court.
  • Two Anointed Ones (v. 14): The legal realization of Zechariah 3:7. Historically, Joshua (Priest) and Zerubbabel (King). Prophetically, the "Two Witnesses" of Revelation 11:3–4, who stood with Council authority during the AD 30–70 transition to bear testimony to the finished work.

4. Annotations: Courtroom Terminology

Might (Chayil) / Power (Koach)

Collective military/social force and individual physical strength. The Divine Council formally strips these elements of their jurisdiction in the building of the New Temple.

Anointed Ones (Bney Ha-Yitshar)

Literally "sons of fresh oil." They are not the source of the oil, but the authorized conduits standing in the Council to mediate the Spirit's supply to the Lampstand (the Church).

Plummet (Ebhen Bedil)

A builder’s weight used to ensure the structure is perfectly vertical. It signifies God’s final judicial assessment and ownership of the New Creation Temple.

5. Courtroom Commentary: The Human Presence in the Council

Zechariah 4 cements the legal revolution begun in Chapter 3. Under the Old Covenant, the Tabernacle lampstand required the daily physical labor of the Levitical priests to trim wicks and crush olives. Here, human effort is completely removed from the supply chain ("Not by might"). Instead, the golden oil flows directly from the Two Anointed Ones. The most critical legal phrase is found in verse 14: these are the ones who "stand by the Lord of the whole earth." This is the exact language used for the celestial members of the Sod. The human representatives (Priest and King) have been elevated into the Divine Council. They now possess celestial standing to administer the "oil" (the Holy Spirit) to the Church on earth. The "Great Mountain" of the old administration—which demanded human performance—was legally declared a "plain" by the Council, paving the way for the spiritual House to be completed by Grace alone.

Standing: Your supply is secured in the Heavenly Court. In the New Creation, your spiritual vitality is not dependent on the "might or power" of human religious systems. The "Great Mountain" that stood between you and God has been legally leveled to a plain. Application: Stop trying to manufacture your own "oil." The pipes are directly connected to the Two Anointed Ones who stand in the Council. Your role is simply to be the "lampstand" that receives the flow of Grace. When you encounter "small things" in your life, do not despise them; the Divine Council is currently measuring your life with the plummet of Grace, and the Capstone has already been securely laid.
