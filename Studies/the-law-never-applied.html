The Law Never Applied to You

Pre-70 the nations stood outside the house. Post-70 the old house is gone. You live in the Last Adam.

People still try to live under the Law. They measure themselves by Sinai, keep a scorecard, and treat the Mosaic administration as if it were a universal moral code that has always applied to everyone and still applies today. It does not. It never did for most people, and it applies to no one now.

The Law was the covenant given to Israel at Sinai. It was not the original problem. The problem was Adam. Sinai came later, to one nation, for a limited time, to expose and increase the trespass within that house. When that house closed, the administration closed with it.

The Law Was Given to Israel

The Mosaic Law is the covenant God made with the descendants of Jacob at Sinai. It is a national covenant document: commandments, statutes, ordinances, priesthood, temple, land, and calendar. Its blessings and curses are addressed to that people in that land under that administration.

“Now therefore, if you will indeed obey my voice and keep my covenant, then you shall be my own possession from among all peoples; for all the earth is mine; and you shall be to me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation.” (Exodus 19:5–6)

Paul is explicit that the Law was added because of transgressions, until the Seed should come, and that it was a guardian until Christ (Galatians 3:19, 24). It was not the foundation of the human story. The foundation is the first Adam and the loss of glory. The Law arrived later as the temporary tutor for one covenant people.

Gentiles Were Never Under It

Before AD 70 the world was not divided into people who kept the Law and people who failed it. It was divided into those inside the Old Covenant house and those outside it. Gentiles were outside. They were not parties to the Sinai covenant. They had no claim on its promises and no standing under its curses.

“Remember that you, once Gentiles in the flesh… were at that time separate from Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers from the covenants of the promise, having no hope and without God in the world.” (Ephesians 2:11–12)

Romans 2:12–14 makes the same distinction: those who sinned without the Law perish without the Law; those who sinned under the Law are judged by the Law. Gentiles were “without the law.” The Law was never their scorecard. They were not under Moses. They were outside the house.

Pre-70: Two Groups, One Problem

Before the old house closed there were two groups:

  • Israel under Moses — inside the Old Covenant administration, under the Law as guardian and ministry of condemnation.
  • Gentiles — outside the covenants of promise, not under the Law, yet still in the Adamic order.

The Law did not apply to the Gentiles. That does not mean they were free of the deeper problem. The problem was never only Sinai. The problem was Adam. Death reigned from Adam. The image was lost. The Law came in to make the trespass increase within the covenant people, but the root issue for the human story is the first man of dust, not the tablets at Sinai.

Christ is the Last Adam. He deals with the root. The cross deactivates the guardian. The old administration loses its claim. The dividing wall comes down. One new man stands where two tracks used to be.

The Guardian Deactivated, the House Closed

The cross is the decisive act. The Law as pedagogue is finished for those in Christ. Paul says we are not under the Law but under grace (Romans 6:14). The ministry of death, written on stone, is being brought to an end even while the physical temple still stands (2 Corinthians 3:7–11). Hebrews 8:13 calls the first covenant obsolete and ready to vanish.

AD 70 is the public close of the old house. The temple falls. The administration that could still accuse and administer the old scorecard is removed. The old covenant world passes. What remains is the New Creation in the Last Adam.

Post-70: New Creation, No Old House

After AD 70 there is no Mosaic administration left to be under. There is no temple court, no Levitical priesthood, no national covenant house applying Sinai to anyone. The people of God are the one new man in Christ. Membership is by faith in the Last Adam, not by DNA and not by keeping the Law.

“There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus.” (Galatians 3:28)

Those in Christ are a new creation. The old has passed away. They bear the image of the heavenly Man, not the man of dust as covenant identity. The Law was never their tutor if they were Gentiles, and it is not their scorecard now. The finished work of the Last Adam is their standing. Inheritance is now. They live in the Eighth Day.

People who try to put themselves or others back under the Law are attempting to rebuild a house that God has already closed. They are measuring New Creation people by an administration that no longer exists and that never applied to the nations in the first place.

Conclusion

The Law never applied to Gentiles before AD 70. They stood outside the house. It applies to no one after AD 70. The old house is gone. The problem was Adam. The Last Adam restored the glory. Christ is the life-giving Spirit. Those in Him are the New Creation, the one new man, living in the open with inheritance now. The scorecard of Sinai has no claim on them. The old world closed. The age to come is the environment they inhabit.
