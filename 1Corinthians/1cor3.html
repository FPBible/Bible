1 Corinthians 3 — God’s Temple, Not Personality Cults

Immediate Context

The factions of chapter 1 are still walking. Paul calls that flesh. Planting and watering are servant work; increase belongs to God. The assembly is God’s field and God’s building. A Day of fire will test the work built on the one foundation, Jesus Christ. Then the sentence that relocates glory: you are God’s temple, and the Spirit lives in you — while another house still stands in Jerusalem.

Scripture (WEB)

1 Corinthians 3 — WEB

6I planted. Apollos watered. But God gave the increase. 9You are God’s farming, God’s building. 11For no one can lay any other foundation than that which has been laid, which is Jesus Christ. 13The Day will declare it, because it is revealed in fire. 16Don’t you know that you are God’s temple and that God’s Spirit lives in you?

Fulfillment Commentary

No one can lay another foundation than Jesus Christ. The Day declares work by fire inside the generation Jesus marked. “You are God’s temple” relocates glory off Zion’s furniture onto the assembly before the stone house falls.

Let no one boast in men. Paul, Apollos, Cephas, the world, life, death, things present, things to come — all are yours. Death is on the list as an item that cannot unseat you, not as a power still running the old accusation. What ends is Death used as a threat-system of the old age — not a still-open Sheol.

You are God’s temple. The Spirit lives in you. Teachers are servants, not owners. What this chapter ends is the need to build your name on a human foundation. The one foundation is already laid.
