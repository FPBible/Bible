1 John 4

1. Immediate Context

John calls the readers to test the spirits because many false prophets have gone out. The decisive test is the confession that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh. God is love; the one who remains in love remains in God. Perfect love casts out fear and gives boldness in the day of judgment.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1 Beloved, don’t believe every spirit, but test the spirits, whether they are of God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world. 2 By this you know the Spirit of God: every spirit who confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is of God, 3 and every spirit who doesn’t confess that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is not of God; and this is the spirit of the Antichrist, of whom you have heard that it comes. Now it is in the world already. 4 You are of God, little children, and have overcome them, because greater is he who is in you than he who is in the world. 5 They are of the world. Therefore they speak of the world, and the world hears them. 6 We are of God. He who knows God listens to us. He who is not of God doesn’t listen to us. By this we know the spirit of truth, and the spirit of error.

7 Beloved, let’s love one another, for love is of God; and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God. 8 He who doesn’t love doesn’t know God, for God is love. 9 By this God’s love was revealed in us, that God has sent his only born Son into the world that we might live through him. 10 In this is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son as the atoning sacrifice for our sins. 11 Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we also ought to love one another. 12 No one has seen God at any time. If we love one another, God remains in us, and his love has been perfected in us.

13 By this we know that we remain in him and he in us, because he has given us of his Spirit. 14 We have seen and testify that the Father has sent the Son as the Savior of the world. 15 Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God remains in him, and he in God. 16 We know and have believed the love which God has for us. God is love, and he who remains in love remains in God, and God remains in him. 17 In this, love has been made perfect among us, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment, because as he is, even so we are in this world. 18 There is no fear in love; but perfect love casts out fear, because fear has punishment. He who fears is not made perfect in love. 19 We love him, because he first loved us. 20 If a man says, “I love God,” and hates his brother, he is a liar; for he who doesn’t love his brother whom he has seen, how can he love God whom he has not seen? 21 This commandment we have from him, that he who loves God should also love his brother.

Source: ebible.org

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • Jesus Christ has come in the flesh (v. 2–3): The decisive confession; denial is the spirit of the Antichrist already in the world.
  • Now it is in the world already (v. 3): The spirit of the Antichrist is present, not merely future.
  • Greater is he who is in you (v. 4): The indwelling One has already given victory over the false spirits.
  • Boldness in the day of judgment (v. 17): Perfected love gives confidence for the day of judgment.

4. Annotations

  • Test the spirits (v. 1): Discernment is required because many false prophets have already gone out.
  • God is love (v. 8, 16): Love is not merely an attribute; it is the nature of God, revealed in the sending of the Son.
  • As he is, even so we are in this world (v. 17): Present identification with Christ is the ground of boldness.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

Test the Spirits (v. 1–6)

Many false prophets have gone out. The test is clear: every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is of God; every spirit that does not is the spirit of the Antichrist, already in the world. The readers have overcome because greater is the One in them than the one in the world. The spirit of truth and the spirit of error are distinguished by who listens to the apostolic testimony.

God Is Love (v. 7–12)

Love is of God; the one who loves is born of God and knows God. God is love. His love was revealed by sending his only Son that we might live through him. Love is not that we loved God but that he loved us and sent his Son as the atoning sacrifice. If God loved in this way, we ought to love one another. Loving one another is the place where God’s love is perfected in us.

Remain in Love · Boldness in Judgment (v. 13–21)

We know we remain in him because he has given of his Spirit. The Father has sent the Son as Savior of the world. Confession that Jesus is the Son of God means mutual remaining. God is love; remaining in love is remaining in God. Perfected love gives boldness in the day of judgment because as he is, so are we in this world. Perfect love casts out fear. We love because he first loved us. Claimed love for God while hating the brother is a lie; the commandment is to love both.

8. New Creation Reality

The confession that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is the settled test. The spirit of the Antichrist has been exposed. Greater is the One in us. We remain in love because he first loved us, and perfected love casts out fear and gives boldness.

9. Reflection

Where does the call to test the spirits by the confession of the incarnation still need to guard you, and where does perfected love still need to cast out fear?
