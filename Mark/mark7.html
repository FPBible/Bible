Mark 7

1. Immediate Context

Pharisees and scribes from Jerusalem confront Jesus over the disciples’ unwashed hands. Jesus charges them with setting aside the commandment of God for the tradition of men. He declares that what defiles a person comes from the heart, not from food. In the region of Tyre a Syrophoenician woman obtains deliverance for her daughter. A deaf man with a speech impediment is healed.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1–13 The Pharisees and scribes fault the disciples for eating with unwashed hands. Jesus cites Isaiah: this people honors me with their lips, but their heart is far from me. They set aside the commandment of God and hold to the tradition of men, making void the word of God by their tradition (e.g., Corban).

14–23 Nothing from outside defiles a person; what proceeds from the heart does. From within come evil thoughts, adulteries, murders, thefts, and the rest. (Thus he declared all foods clean.)

24–30 In the region of Tyre a Greek Syrophoenician woman begs for her daughter. Jesus speaks of the children’s bread; she answers that even the dogs eat the crumbs. For this saying the demon is gone.

31–37 In Decapolis a deaf man with a speech impediment is healed. “Ephphatha!” — Be opened. They say, “He has done all things well.”

Source: ebible.org

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • Making void the word of God by your tradition (v. 13): The old order’s human traditions nullify the commandment.
  • Making all foods clean (v. 19): The ceremonial food laws are set aside by Jesus’ word.
  • Even the dogs under the table (v. 28): Gentile faith receives the crumbs; the mission already reaches beyond Israel.

4. Annotations

  • Corban (v. 11): A vow that dedicated resources to God and thereby excused support of parents.
  • Ephphatha (v. 34): Aramaic preserved; the command that opens.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

The conflict with tradition reaches a climax. Human commandments have replaced God’s word. Jesus relocates defilement from the external (food, washings) to the heart. In the same movement he grants the request of a Gentile woman and opens the ears of the deaf. The old purity system is being dismantled; the Kingdom’s reach is already wider than the traditions allowed.

8. New Creation Reality

All foods are clean. Defilement is a matter of the heart. Gentile faith has already received the crumbs that become a feast. We live free from the traditions that voided the word of God.

9. Reflection

Where do human traditions still compete with the word of God, and where does the heart still need the cleansing that food laws could never provide?
