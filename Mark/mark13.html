Mark 13

1. Immediate Context

Leaving the temple, Jesus predicts that not one stone will be left upon another. On the Mount of Olives the disciples ask when these things will be and what the sign will be. Jesus answers with warnings, the abomination of desolation, great oppression, cosmic signs, and the coming of the Son of Man in clouds. He closes with the solemn assurance: this generation will not pass away until all these things happen.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1–2 “Do you see these great buildings? There will not be left here one stone on another, which will not be thrown down.”

3–4 On the Mount of Olives opposite the temple: “Tell us, when will these things be? What is the sign that these things are all about to be fulfilled?”

5–13 Do not be led astray. Wars and rumors of wars; the end is not yet. Birth pains. Delivered to councils, beaten in synagogues, standing before rulers. The Good News must first be preached to all the nations. Brother will deliver up brother. Hated for my name’s sake; the one who endures to the end will be saved.

14–23 When you see the abomination of desolation standing where it ought not (let the reader understand), then those in Judea must flee to the mountains. Great oppression, such as has not been from the beginning of creation. For the sake of the chosen ones the days are shortened. False christs and false prophets will arise. “Behold, I have told you all things beforehand.”

24–27 After that oppression: the sun darkened, the moon not giving light, stars falling, powers in the heavens shaken. Then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory. He will send his angels and gather his chosen ones from the four winds.

28–31 From the fig tree learn: when you see these things coming to pass, know that it is near, at the doors. Most certainly I say to you, this generation will not pass away until all these things happen. Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away.

32–37 Of that day or hour no one knows — not even the angels, nor the Son, but only the Father. Watch, keep alert, and pray. What I tell you, I tell all: Watch!

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • Not one stone on another (v. 2): The temple’s complete destruction is predicted; fulfilled in AD 70.
  • This generation will not pass away until all these things happen (v. 30): The decisive time statement. All the things of the discourse — including the coming of the Son of Man in clouds — are bound to the generation then living.
  • It is near, at the doors (v. 29): When the signs are seen, the fulfillment is imminent for that generation.
  • The Son of Man coming in clouds (v. 26): Daniel 7 language of vindication and authority, enacted in the judgment that ended the old order.

4. Annotations

  • Abomination of desolation (v. 14): Daniel’s phrase; the signal for flight from Judea. “Let the reader understand” points to a recognisable historical referent.
  • This generation (v. 30): The same generation addressed throughout the Gospel (8:12, 8:38, 9:19); not a distant future race.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

The disciples’ question is about the temple’s destruction and the sign of its fulfillment. Jesus’ answer stays within that frame. Wars, earthquakes, famines, persecution, the abomination, flight from Judea, unparalleled oppression, cosmic signs, and the coming of the Son of Man in clouds are all placed within the lifetime of “this generation.” The temple fell in AD 70. The old covenant order was judged. The Son of Man came in the clouds of heaven in the sense of Daniel 7 — receiving dominion and executing the judgment announced against the unfaithful tenants (12:9). Heaven and earth (the old order’s stability) passed away; his words did not. The call to watch was a call to that generation; it remains a call to readiness for all who live under the finished work.

8. New Creation Reality

This generation did not pass away until all these things happened. The temple is gone. The old order has been judged. The Son of Man has come in the clouds with power and glory. We live in the age that followed that coming — the New Covenant reality in which his words remain and the call is still to watch.
