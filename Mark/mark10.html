Mark 10

1. Immediate Context

On the way to Jerusalem Jesus teaches on marriage and divorce, receives little children, challenges a rich man, predicts his passion a third time, and redefines greatness as servanthood. The Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many. Bartimaeus receives his sight and follows on the way.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1–12 Question on divorce. From the beginning God made them male and female; the two become one flesh. What God has joined, let no one separate.

13–16 Little children are brought. “Allow the little children to come to me… God’s Kingdom belongs to such as these.”

17–31 A rich man asks about eternal life. “One thing you lack… sell… give… follow me.” He goes away sorrowful. How hard for those who trust in riches to enter the Kingdom. With God all things are possible. Those who have left all will receive a hundredfold now, with persecutions, and in the age to come eternal life.

32–34 Going up to Jerusalem, Jesus predicts: the Son of Man will be delivered, condemned, mocked, spat on, scourged, killed; on the third day he will rise.

35–45 James and John request seats in glory. “The Son of Man also came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”

46–52 Bartimaeus, son of Timaeus, cries out, “Jesus, you son of David, have mercy on me!” His faith makes him well; he follows on the way.

Source: ebible.org

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • On the third day he will rise again (v. 34): The third passion prediction; the path is fixed.
  • A ransom for many (v. 45): The purpose of the coming is substitutionary service culminating in the cross.
  • In the age to come eternal life (v. 30): Present hundredfold with persecutions; eternal life in the age that was then coming.

4. Annotations

  • Ransom for many (v. 45): The climactic statement of the purpose of the Son of Man’s mission.
  • Son of David (v. 47–48): Messianic title on the lips of the blind beggar.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

The road to Jerusalem is the road of the cross. Marriage is restored to the creation pattern. Children are welcomed. Riches are a hindrance when trusted. The third passion prediction is the most detailed. Greatness is servanthood; the Son of Man came to give his life as a ransom for many. Bartimaeus sees and follows. The ransom is about to be paid; the many are about to be redeemed.

8. New Creation Reality

The ransom has been paid. The many have been redeemed. We follow the Son of Man who came not to be served but to serve, on the way that leads through the cross to resurrection life.

9. Reflection

Where does the call to be servant of all, after the pattern of the One who gave his life as a ransom, still need to reorder your ambition?
