Mark 16

1. Immediate Context

Very early on the first day of the week the women come to the tomb. The stone is rolled away. A young man in white announces that Jesus has risen and goes before them into Galilee. He appears to Mary Magdalene, to two on the road, and to the eleven. He commissions them to preach the Good News to the whole creation. He is received up into heaven and sits at the right hand of God. They go out and preach everywhere, the Lord working with them.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1–8 The women come to the tomb. The stone is rolled back. A young man in a white robe: “Don’t be amazed. You seek Jesus, the Nazarene, who has been crucified. He has risen! He is not here… Go, tell his disciples and Peter, ‘He goes before you into Galilee. There you will see him, as he said to you.’” They flee, trembling and astonished.

9–14 He appears first to Mary Magdalene; the disciples disbelieve. He is revealed to two on the way; they are not believed. He is revealed to the eleven and rebukes their unbelief and hardness of heart.

15–18 “Go into all the world and preach the Good News to the whole creation. He who believes and is baptized will be saved; but he who disbelieves will be condemned.” Signs will accompany those who believe.

19–20 The Lord is received up into heaven and sits at the right hand of God. They go out and preach everywhere, the Lord working with them and confirming the word by the signs that followed.

Source: ebible.org

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • He has risen! (v. 6): The third-day prediction is fulfilled. The tomb is empty.
  • He goes before you into Galilee (v. 7): The word spoken before the cross (14:28) is kept.
  • Received up into heaven and sat down at the right hand of God (v. 19): The Son of Man is at the right hand of Power, as he confessed before the high priest (14:62).

4. Annotations

  • Tell his disciples and Peter (v. 7): Peter is named specifically after the denial; restoration is already in view.
  • The whole creation (v. 15): The scope of the Good News matches the scope of the new creation.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

The beginning of the Good News (1:1) ends with the empty tomb and the risen Lord. He has risen as he said. He goes before them into Galilee as he said. He is received up and sits at the right hand of God — the position from which he comes with the clouds (14:62; 13:26). The disciples go out and preach; the Lord works with them. The Gospel that began with “the time is fulfilled, the Kingdom is at hand” ends with the risen King at the right hand of God and the Good News going into all the world. The generation that heard “this generation will not pass away” lived to see the risen Lord, the outpouring of the Spirit, the preaching to the nations, and the judgment that left not one stone of the temple upon another.

8. New Creation Reality

He has risen. He is at the right hand of God. The Good News is for the whole creation. We live as those who believe and are baptized, under the Lord who works with his people and confirms his word, in the age that followed the coming of the Son of Man in the clouds of heaven.

9. Reflection
