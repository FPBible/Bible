Mark 15

1. Immediate Context

Jesus is delivered to Pilate, rejected in favour of Barabbas, mocked by the soldiers, and crucified at Golgotha. Darkness covers the land. He cries out the opening of Psalm 22 and gives up the spirit. The veil of the temple is torn from top to bottom. The centurion confesses, “Truly this man was the Son of God.” Joseph of Arimathaea buries him.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1–15 Before Pilate: “Are you the King of the Jews?” “So you say.” Barabbas is released; Jesus is handed over to be crucified.

16–20 The soldiers clothe him in purple, crown him with thorns, salute him, strike him, and lead him out to crucify him.

21–32 Simon of Cyrene bears the cross. At Golgotha he is crucified; the superscription: “THE KING OF THE JEWS.” Two robbers with him. Mockery: “He saved others. He can’t save himself.”

33–39 Darkness from the sixth to the ninth hour. “Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani?” He cries out and gives up the spirit. The veil of the temple is torn in two from top to bottom. The centurion: “Truly this man was the Son of God!”

40–47 The women watch from afar. Joseph of Arimathaea asks for the body, wraps it in linen, and lays it in a rock-hewn tomb. A stone is rolled against the door.

Source: ebible.org

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • The King of the Jews (v. 26): The charge that is true is written over the cross.
  • The veil of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom (v. 38): Access is opened; the old sanctuary’s barrier is removed by God from above.
  • Truly this man was the Son of God (v. 39): The Gentile centurion confesses what the leaders denied.

4. Annotations

  • Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani (v. 34): Psalm 22:1; the righteous sufferer’s cry that ends in vindication.
  • From top to bottom (v. 38): The direction indicates divine action, not human vandalism.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

The ransom is paid. The King of the Jews is crucified. Darkness covers the land. The cry from the cross is the cry of the righteous sufferer. The veil is torn from top to bottom — the way into the presence of God is opened, and the old order’s central barrier is finished. The centurion sees and confesses. The body is laid in the tomb. The third day is still to come, but the work of the cross is complete.

8. New Creation Reality

The veil is torn. The way is open. The Son of God has given his life as a ransom for many. We enter with boldness by the new and living way he has consecrated for us.
