Mark 5

1. Immediate Context

Jesus crosses to the country of the Gadarenes and confronts a man with an unclean spirit named Legion. The demons are cast into a herd of pigs. Returning, he is met by Jairus, whose daughter is dying. On the way a woman with a twelve-year flow of blood is healed by touching his garment. Jairus’s daughter is raised from the dead.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1 They came to the other side of the sea, into the country of the Gadarenes. 2 When he had come out of the boat, immediately a man with an unclean spirit met him out of the tombs. 3 He lived in the tombs. Nobody could bind him any more, not even with chains, 4 because he had been often bound with fetters and chains, and the chains had been torn apart by him, and the fetters broken in pieces. Nobody had the strength to tame him. 5 Always, night and day, in the tombs and in the mountains, he was crying out, and cutting himself with stones. 6 When he saw Jesus from afar, he ran and bowed down to him, 7 and crying out with a loud voice, he said, “What have I to do with you, Jesus, you Son of the Most High God? I adjure you by God, don’t torment me.” 8 For he said to him, “Come out of the man, you unclean spirit!” 9 He asked him, “What is your name?” He said to him, “My name is Legion, for we are many.” 10 He begged him much that he would not send them away out of the country. 11 Now on the mountainside there was a great herd of pigs feeding. 12 All the demons begged him, saying, “Send us into the pigs, that we may enter into them.” 13 At once Jesus gave them permission. The unclean spirits came out and entered into the pigs. The herd of about two thousand rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and they were drowned in the sea.

14–20 The people beg him to depart. The delivered man is sent to proclaim in Decapolis what the Lord has done for him.

21–24 Jairus, a ruler of the synagogue, falls at Jesus’ feet and begs him to come and lay hands on his dying daughter.

25–34 A woman with a twelve-year discharge of blood touches his clothes and is immediately healed. Jesus tells her, “Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace.”

35–43 News comes that the daughter is dead. Jesus says, “Don’t be afraid, only believe.” He takes the child’s hand and says, “Talitha cumi!” She rises and walks. He commands that something be given her to eat.

Source: ebible.org

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • Son of the Most High God (v. 7): The demons know and confess his identity.
  • Authority over Legion (v. 8–13): A multitude of unclean spirits are expelled with a word; the strong man’s house is plundered.
  • Your faith has made you well (v. 34): Faith receives the power already present in Jesus.
  • The child is not dead, but is asleep (v. 39): Authority extends over death itself.

4. Annotations

  • Legion (v. 9): A Roman military term for a large force; the oppression is massive yet yields immediately.
  • Talitha cumi (v. 41): Aramaic preserved; the intimate authority of the command.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

Across the sea Jesus confronts a man no chains could hold. Legion is expelled. The delivered man becomes a proclaimer in Decapolis. Returning, Jesus is interrupted by a desperate father and a desperate woman. Both receive according to faith. The woman is healed of chronic uncleanness; the girl is raised from death. Authority over the demonic, over uncleanness, and over death is demonstrated in a single movement.

8. New Creation Reality

The strong man is bound. Uncleanness is cleansed. Death is reversed. We live under the authority of the One who speaks and Legion flees, who is touched and power goes out, who takes the dead by the hand and raises them.

9. Reflection

Where does the authority that expelled Legion and raised the dead still need to meet the places of oppression or fear in your life?
