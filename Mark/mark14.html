Mark 14

1. Immediate Context

Two days before the Passover the chief priests and scribes seek to seize Jesus by deception. A woman anoints him for burial. Judas agrees to betray him. At the Passover meal Jesus institutes the new covenant in his blood. In Gethsemane he prays that the cup might pass, yet submits to the Father’s will. He is arrested, tried before the high priest, and confesses that they will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power and coming with the clouds of the sky. Peter denies him three times.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1–11 The plot to seize Jesus. A woman anoints him with costly nard. “She has anointed my body beforehand for the burying.” Judas goes to the chief priests to deliver him.

12–26 The Passover is prepared. “One of you will betray me.” He takes bread: “This is my body.” He takes the cup: “This is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many. I will no more drink of the fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it anew in God’s Kingdom.”

27–31 “All of you will be made to stumble… After I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee.” Peter’s threefold denial is predicted.

32–42 Gethsemane. “Abba, Father… remove this cup from me. However, not what I desire, but what you desire.” The disciples sleep. “The hour has come. The Son of Man is betrayed into the hands of sinners.”

43–52 Judas arrives with a multitude. The kiss. Jesus is seized. “This is so that the Scriptures might be fulfilled.” All flee.

53–65 Before the high priest. False testimony about destroying the temple. “Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?” Jesus: “I am. You will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming with the clouds of the sky.” They condemn him as worthy of death.

66–72 Peter’s three denials. The rooster crows the second time. He weeps.

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • My blood of the new covenant, poured out for many (v. 24): The new covenant is inaugurated in his blood; the ransom for many (10:45) is about to be paid.
  • You will see the Son of Man… coming with the clouds (v. 62): Spoken to the high priest and the council — they themselves will see the vindication and coming of the Son of Man.
  • The Scriptures might be fulfilled (v. 49): The arrest is not chaos; it is the path written.

4. Annotations

  • Anointed beforehand for the burying (v. 8): The woman’s act is interpreted as preparation for death.
  • Abba, Father (v. 36): The intimate address in the hour of the cup.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

The new covenant is declared in the cup. The Son of Man goes as it is written. In Gethsemane the will of the Father is embraced. Before the council Jesus does not remain silent on the decisive question: he is the Christ, the Son of the Blessed, and they will see the Son of Man at the right hand of Power and coming with the clouds. The same generation that condemns him is told that they will see his vindication. The path of the cross is the path to the clouds of glory.

8. New Creation Reality

The new covenant has been inaugurated in his blood. The Son of Man has been vindicated and has come with the clouds. We live under the finished covenant, drinking the cup of the Kingdom that he drinks anew with his own.

9. Reflection

How does the confession before the high priest — “You will see the Son of Man… coming with the clouds” — confirm that the coming was bound to the generation that condemned him?
