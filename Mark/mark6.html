Mark 6

1. Immediate Context

Jesus is rejected in his own country. He sends the Twelve two by two with authority over unclean spirits. Herod, hearing of the works, fears John has risen. The narrative recounts John’s beheading. The apostles return; Jesus feeds five thousand, walks on the sea, and heals many in Gennesaret.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1–6 Jesus teaches in the synagogue of his own country. They are offended at the carpenter, the son of Mary. He could do no mighty work there because of their unbelief.

7–13 He sends the Twelve two by two, giving them authority over unclean spirits. They preach repentance, cast out demons, and heal.

14–29 Herod hears and says John has risen. The account of John’s arrest and beheading at Herodias’s request is given.

30–44 The apostles return. Jesus has compassion on the crowd like sheep without a shepherd. With five loaves and two fish he feeds five thousand men; twelve baskets of fragments remain.

45–52 He walks on the sea. The disciples are terrified; he says, “Cheer up! It is I! Don’t be afraid.” The wind ceases. Their hearts were hardened; they had not understood about the loaves.

53–56 In Gennesaret the sick are brought; as many as touch the fringe of his garment are made well.

Source: ebible.org

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • Authority given to the Twelve (v. 7): The same authority Jesus exercises is shared with those he sends.
  • Sheep without a shepherd (v. 34): The compassion of the true Shepherd for the scattered people.
  • It is I (v. 50): Echo of the divine name; the One who walks on the sea is the I AM.

4. Annotations

  • Shake off the dust (v. 11): A testimony against those who refuse the messengers of the Kingdom.
  • Hearts were hardened (v. 52): Even the disciples have not yet understood the significance of the loaves.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

Rejection at home does not stop the mission. The Twelve are sent with authority. John’s death at Herod’s hand prefigures the cost of the prophetic witness. Jesus feeds the multitude as the true Shepherd, walks on the sea as Lord over chaos, and continues to heal. The Kingdom advances through rejection, sending, compassion, and power.

8. New Creation Reality

Authority has been shared. The Shepherd has had compassion. The I AM has walked on the sea. We live under the same Lord who feeds, sends, and stills the storm.

9. Reflection

Where does unbelief still limit what the Lord would do, and where is he still saying, “Cheer up! It is I! Don’t be afraid”?
