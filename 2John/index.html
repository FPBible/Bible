2 John

Walk in Truth and Love · Guard the Teaching

This brief letter from the elder is a pastoral warning written inside the final decades of the old covenant age. The chosen lady and her children are called to continue walking in the truth they already received, to love one another, and to refuse any teaching that denies Jesus Christ came in the flesh.

The letter is short because the truth is already settled. The task is not to invent something new, but to remain in what has been given from the beginning.
