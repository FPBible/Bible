Preface — How to Read 2 John

2 John is a short pastoral letter written in the closing decades of the old covenant age. The elder writes to the “chosen lady and her children” — most likely a local assembly and its members — urging them to continue in the truth they already possess.

The letter is not introducing a new revelation. It is a call to remain. The truth has already been given. Love has already been commanded. The danger is not ignorance but departure.

What this letter shows

  • The central confession is that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. Any teaching that denies this is antichrist.
  • Love is defined as walking according to the commandments already received from the beginning.
  • Hospitality is not unconditional. Welcoming a teacher who brings a different gospel makes one a partner in that evil work.
  • Remaining in the teaching of Christ means having both the Father and the Son.

How to read it

Read 2 John as a letter written to people living in the overlapping period before the full end of the old order. The elder assumes the truth is already settled. The task is perseverance in what has been received, not the invention of new systems. The same call continues: remain in the teaching of Christ.
