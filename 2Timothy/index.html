2 Timothy

Guard the Deposit · Finish the Course · The Last Days

Paul’s final letter, written from prison as the time of his departure has come. He charges Timothy to stir up the gift, endure hardship, hold the pattern of sound words, and remain in the things he has learned. The last days of the old covenant age are marked by grievous times, yet God’s firm foundation stands.

This is not a letter of despair. It is the settled confidence of a man who has fought the good fight, finished the course, and kept the faith — looking to the crown that the righteous Judge will give on that day.
