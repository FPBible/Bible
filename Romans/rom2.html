Romans 2

The Judge Judged, the Law Exposed

1. Immediate Context

From open idolatry Paul turns to those who judge others while relying on Law, circumcision, and the name “Jew.” Possession of Torah is treated as shelter. Romans 2 strips that shelter: doers, not hearers, are justified; God shows no favoritism; the true Jew is marked in the heart by the Spirit. This is covenant litigation inside Israel’s last generation, not an attack on Jewish people as people.

2. Scripture (WEB)

Source (verbatim): https://ebible.org/web/ROM02.htm

1 Therefore you are without excuse, O man, whoever you are who judge. For in that which you judge another, you condemn yourself. For you who judge practice the same things.
2 We know that the judgment of God is according to truth against those who practice such things.
3 Do you think this, O man who judges those who practice such things, and do the same, that you will escape the judgment of God?
4 Or do you despise the riches of his goodness, forbearance, and patience, not knowing that the goodness of God leads you to repentance?
5 But according to your hardness and unrepentant heart you are treasuring up for yourself wrath in the day of wrath, revelation, and of the righteous judgment of God,
6 who “will pay back to everyone according to their works:”
7 to those who by perseverance in well-doing seek for glory, honor, and incorruptibility, eternal life;
8 but to those who are self-seeking and don’t obey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, will be wrath, indignation,
9 oppression, and anguish on every soul of man who does evil, to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.
10 But glory, honor, and peace go to every man who does good, to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.
11 For there is no partiality with God.
12 For as many as have sinned without the law will also perish without the law. As many as have sinned under the law will be judged by the law.
13 For it isn’t the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but the doers of the law will be justified
14 (for when Gentiles who don’t have the law do by nature the things of the law, these, not having the law, are a law to themselves,
15 in that they show the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience testifying with them, and their thoughts among themselves accusing or else excusing them)
16 in the day when God will judge the secrets of men, according to my Good News, by Jesus Christ.
17 Indeed you bear the name of a Jew, rest on the law, glory in God,
18 know his will, and approve the things that are excellent, being instructed out of the law,
19 and are confident that you yourself are a guide of the blind, a light to those who are in darkness,
20 a corrector of the foolish, a teacher of babies, having in the law the form of knowledge and of the truth.
21 You therefore who teach another, don’t you teach yourself? You who preach that a man shouldn’t steal, do you steal?
22 You who say a man shouldn’t commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?
23 You who glory in the law, do you dishonor God by disobeying the law?
24 For “the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you,” just as it is written.
25 For circumcision indeed profits, if you are a doer of the law, but if you are a transgressor of the law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.
26 If therefore the uncircumcised keep the ordinances of the law, won’t his uncircumcision be accounted as circumcision?
27 Won’t those who are physically uncircumcised, but fulfill the law, judge you, who with the letter and circumcision are a transgressor of the law?
28 For he is not a Jew who is one outwardly, neither is that circumcision which is outward in the flesh;
29 but he is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, not in the letter; whose praise is not from men, but from God.

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • Audience: The one who judges (v. 1) is the covenant insider using Torah as a mirror for others and a shield for himself.
  • No favoritism: “There is no partiality with God” (v. 11) ends genealogical shelter in the courtroom of that age.
  • Heart circumcision: “Circumcision is of the heart, in the spirit, not in the letter” (v. 29) is Jeremiah 31 / Deuteronomy 30 arriving, not a later program.
  • Name blasphemed: Isaiah 52:5 is happening among the nations in Paul’s day — privilege without faithfulness.

4. Annotations

  • Hearers vs doers — possessing Torah is not covenant faithfulness.
  • Jew inwardly — identity relocated from flesh-sign to Spirit-work.
  • The day of wrath — the same first-century reckoning already unveiled in chapter 1, not a generic end of the universe.
  • Law as boast — the old world’s badge; it cannot cover the one who dishonours God by means of it.

5. Study Commentary

Privilege is not righteousness

Romans 2 is not anti-Jewish. It is anti-privilege-as-righteousness. The tragedy of the old covenant’s last days is a people entrusted with the oracles of God who used those oracles to judge the nations while remaining unrepentant themselves. Jesus had already named this in Matthew 23. Paul now puts it in courtroom prose.

When the name of God is blasphemed among the nations because of those who carry the Law (v. 24), the old system is already on trial. AD 70 does not invent that trial. It publishes the verdict on a house that would not come to the light.

Doers, not hearers — and the doers will be defined by the Spirit

“Not the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but the doers.” That sentence is often turned into a new performance religion. Paul is not smuggling Sinai back in. He is stripping the claim that hearing Torah equals standing. The later chapters will show that the only “doing” that survives is the obedience of faith produced in those who receive the Spirit.

Gentiles who do by nature the things of the Law (vv. 14–15) are not a rival Torah. They are evidence that God is already writing what the Law required on hearts among the nations — the New Covenant promise arriving without the Jerusalem badge.

Circumcision of the heart is the New Covenant people

Deuteronomy 30 promised a circumcision God would perform. Jeremiah 9 warned against boasting in flesh-circumcision. Ezekiel 36 promised a new heart and Spirit. Romans 2:28–29 gathers those lines and locates them in the present people of the Spirit.

The true Jew, in Paul’s argument, is not postponed Israel of a later millennium. It is the person whose praise is from God, not from men — the remnant and the grafted nations who share Abraham’s faith. Chapter 2 clears special pleading so chapter 3 can silence every mouth.

The day of wrath in that generation

Paul’s “day of wrath and revelation of the righteous judgment of God” (v. 5) sits inside the same near horizon as the rest of the letter: the gospel already going out, the old world already being handed over, the public end of the temple order approaching.

New Creation identity does not hide in heritage, denomination, or knowledge. It lives from heart-circumcision in the Spirit. The old badge is not the doorway.
