Romans 13

Order in the New Creation World

1. Immediate Context

Romans 13 continues the call to live faithfully while a covenant world undergoes upheaval. Paul addresses how New Creation communities related to existing authorities as judgment gathered over the old house. He then returns to love as the Law’s fulfillment, and to time: it is already the hour to wake; the night is far gone; the day has drawn near. That is first-century dawn language, not a perpetual postponement.

2. Scripture (WEB)

Source (verbatim): https://ebible.org/web/ROM13.htm

1 Let every soul be in subjection to the higher authorities, for there is no authority except from God, and those who exist are ordained by God.
2 Therefore he who resists the authority withstands the ordinance of God; and those who withstand will receive to themselves judgment.
3 For rulers are not a terror to the good work, but to the evil. Do you desire to have no fear of the authority? Do that which is good, and you will have praise from the authority,
4 for he is a servant of God to you for good. But if you do that which is evil, be afraid, for he doesn’t bear the sword in vain; for he is a servant of God, an avenger for wrath to him who does evil.
5 Therefore you need to be in subjection, not only because of the wrath, but also for conscience’ sake.
6 For this reason you also pay taxes, for they are servants of God’s service, continually doing this very thing.
7 Therefore give everyone what you owe: if you owe taxes, pay taxes; if customs, then customs; if respect, then respect; if honor, then honor.
8 Owe no one anything, except to love one another; for he who loves his neighbor has fulfilled the law.
9 For the commandments, “You shall not commit adultery,” “You shall not murder,” “You shall not steal,” “You shall not covet,” and whatever other commandments there are, are all summed up in this saying, namely, “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”
10 Love doesn’t harm a neighbor. Love therefore is the fulfillment of the law.
11 Do this, knowing the time, that it is already time for you to awaken out of sleep, for salvation is now nearer to us than when we first believed.
12 The night is far gone, and the day is near. Let’s therefore throw off the deeds of darkness, and let’s put on the armor of light.
13 Let’s walk properly, as in the day; not in reveling and drunkenness, not in sexual promiscuity and lustful acts, and not in strife and jealousy.
14 But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, for its lusts.

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • Authorities in that moment (vv. 1–7) — real magistrates in a volatile empire, not a timeless theology of every later state.
  • Wrath (v. 4) — the sword as historical judgment-tool, the same wrath already unveiled in ch. 1.
  • Love fulfills the Law (vv. 8–10) — the Law’s goal reached in the new people, so Torah as administration is complete.
  • The day at hand (vv. 11–14) — near-time language for that generation’s dawn, not “always one day away.”

4. Annotations

  • Minister of God — the ruler as a servant in God’s ordering of the moment, not a messiah.
  • Owe no one anything except love — the remaining debt of the new world.
  • Put on the Lord Jesus Christ — clothing of the new day.
  • Provision for the flesh — the old night’s habits.

5. Study Commentary

This is not a later political theory dressed as prophecy

Paul tells assemblies in the 50s–60s not to become a riot inside Rome’s order while they wait for the Lord’s public vindication of the gospel. The authorities bear the sword as avengers of wrong in ordinary life. That is a far cry from baptising every later empire, and a far cry from Zealot revolt as holiness.

The churches’ warfare is not against flesh and blood in the streets of Rome. Their hope is not a coup. Their hope is the dawn God has already begun.

Love is what the Law was for

He who loves the other has fulfilled the Law. The commandments are summed in “love your neighbour as yourself.” Love does no harm. Therefore love is the fullness of the Law.

Once that is said, Sinai’s job as the life-system of a nation is complete. You do not keep the old calendar in order to finish what love has already named. You walk in love because the day has come.

The night is far gone

Knowing the time — that it is already the hour to wake from sleep — salvation nearer than when we first believed — the night far gone, the day at hand. Futurism stretches those words until they mean nothing. Paul meant them.

The old age’s night is ending in that generation. Casting off works of darkness and putting on the armour of light is how a dawn-people live while the temple still casts a shadow. AD 70 is high noon for the old house. The churches are told to live as those who already see the light.

Put on Christ, not the night

Make no provision for the flesh. Put on the Lord Jesus Christ. Identity is clothing. You wear the day you belong to.

New Creation people do not need to become the sword. They walk honestly as in the day, because the day has already drawn near and, in the Messiah, has already begun.

  • The sword — historical wrath through human order.
  • Fulfill the law — love as completion, not addition.
  • The day at hand — near dawn of that generation.

9. New Creation Reality

You live after the night has begun to lift. You do not resist ordinary order as if revolt were holiness, and you do not fear as if the night were the last word. You put on Christ because the day has already drawn near.

10. Reflection

Where might I still read Scripture as if the night were ongoing, instead of living in the daylight already revealed?
