1 Timothy 2

1. Immediate Context

Paul turns from the charge against false teachers to the public life of the assembly. First of all, prayers are to be made for all people and for kings, so that a quiet and godly life may be lived. The theological foundation is the one God and the one mediator, the man Christ Jesus, who gave himself as a ransom for all. Instructions follow for the conduct of men and women in the gathering.

2. Scripture (WEB)

1 I exhort therefore, first of all, that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and givings of thanks be made for all men, 2 for kings and all who are in high places, that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and reverence. 3 For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Savior, 4 who desires all people to be saved and come to full knowledge of the truth. 5 For there is one God and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus, 6 who gave himself as a ransom for all, the testimony at the proper time, 7 to which I was appointed a preacher and an apostle—I am telling the truth in Christ, not lying—a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.

8 I desire therefore that the men in every place pray, lifting up holy hands without anger and doubting. 9 In the same way, that women also adorn themselves in decent clothing, with modesty and propriety, not with braided hair, gold, pearls, or expensive clothing, 10 but with good works, which is appropriate for women professing godliness. 11 Let a woman learn in quietness with full submission. 12 But I don’t permit a woman to teach, nor to exercise authority over a man, but to be in quietness. 13 For Adam was formed first, then Eve. 14 Adam wasn’t deceived, but the woman, being deceived, has fallen into disobedience; 15 but she will be saved through her childbearing, if they continue in faith, love, and holiness with sobriety.

Source: ebible.org

3. Fulfillment Notes

  • One God and one mediator (v. 5): The exclusive mediation of the man Christ Jesus is the settled centre of the New Covenant.
  • Ransom for all, the testimony at the proper time (v. 6): The giving of himself has already occurred at the appointed time.
  • Teacher of the Gentiles (v. 7): Paul’s apostleship to the nations is already in operation inside the transition.
  • Desires all people to be saved (v. 4): The scope of the desire matches the scope of the ransom already given.

4. Annotations

  • Tranquil and quiet life (v. 2): The practical aim of prayer for rulers is space for godly living.
  • Holy hands without anger and doubting (v. 8): The posture of prayer is to be free from the disputes that characterised the false teachers.
  • Saved through her childbearing (v. 15): Most naturally read in connection with the promise of the Seed through the woman (Genesis 3:15) and the call to continue in faith, love, and holiness.

5. Fulfillment Commentary

Prayer for All · One Mediator (v. 1–7)

First of all, prayers of every kind are to be made for all people and for those in authority. This is good and acceptable to God our Savior, who desires all to be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth. The foundation is the exclusive claim: one God, one mediator — the man Christ Jesus — who gave himself as a ransom for all. The testimony has already been given at the proper time. Paul is the appointed preacher, apostle, and teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.

Men and Women in the Assembly (v. 8–15)

Men are to pray in every place, lifting holy hands without anger or disputing. Women are to adorn themselves with modesty and good works rather than costly display. Paul does not permit a woman to teach or exercise authority over a man in the assembly, grounding the instruction in the creation order and the deception of the woman. The final word points to salvation in connection with childbearing, conditioned on continuing in faith, love, and holiness with sobriety.

There is one God and one mediator. The ransom has been given. Prayer is offered for all. We live under the finished mediation of the man Christ Jesus, in the quiet and godly life that the truth produces.
