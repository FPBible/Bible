Lesson 1: Outgrowing the Guardian

第一課：脫離監護人

"Therefore the law was our tutor (guardian) to bring us to Christ... But after faith has come, we are no longer under a tutor." — Galatians 3:24-25

「這樣，律法是我們訓蒙的師傅（監護人），引我們到基督那裡... 但這因信得救的理既然來到，我們從此就不在師傅的手下了。」— 加拉太書 3:24-25

1. The Core Truth / 核心真理

In the Old Covenant, God's people were treated like minors. The Law was their strict "guardian" meant to supervise them until they grew up. Christ came to graduate us from the guardian and give us the full rights of adult sons.

在舊約中，神的子民如同未成年人。律法是他們嚴格的「監護人」，負責在他們長大前看管他們。基督來，是為了讓我們從監護人手中畢業，並賜予我們成年兒子完全的權利。

2. The Eighth Day Reality / 第八日的真實

The destruction of the Temple in AD 70 wasn't just a tragedy; it was the final, historical end of the "Guardian" system. The Old Age is completely over. We are no longer slaves waiting for freedom; we are living in the New Creation right now.

西元 70 年聖殿的毀滅不僅是一場悲劇；它在歷史上徹底終結了「監護人」系統。舊時代已經完全過去。我們不再是等待自由的奴僕；我們此刻正活在新造的現實中。

3. Talk About It / 討論與分享

Where in your life are you still trying to "follow rules" to earn the Father's love, instead of resting in your identity as a son?

在你生活中的哪些方面，你仍在試圖「遵守規則」以贏得天父的愛，而不是安息在你兒子的身份中？
