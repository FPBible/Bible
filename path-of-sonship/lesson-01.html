Lesson 1: The Two Types of Slavery

第一課：兩種奴役的狀態

"But before faith came, we were kept under guard by the law... Therefore the law was our tutor (guardian) to bring us to Christ." (Galatians 3:23-24)

「但這因信得救的理還未來以先，我們被看守在律法之下... 這樣，律法是我們訓蒙的師傅（監護人），引我們到基督那裡。」（加拉太書 3:23-24）

"So you are no longer a slave, but a son... Formerly, when you did not know God, you were enslaved to those that by nature are not gods." (Galatians 4:7-8)

「可見，從此以後，你不是奴僕，乃是兒子了... 但從前你們不認識神的時候，是給那些本來不是神的神作奴僕。」（加拉太書 4:7-8）

I. The Jewish Experience: Under the Guardian / 猶太人的經歷：在監護人之下

Before the New Covenant, the whole world lacked the full rights of mature sonship, but they experienced this lack in two distinct ways. For the Jews, they were inside God's covenant family, but treated as minors. Paul calls the Mosaic Law a paidagogos—a strict "guardian" or disciplinarian. The Law was holy, but it was restrictive. It kept the Old Covenant people in a state of spiritual immaturity, focusing on outward rituals, sacrifices, and a deep awareness of their distance from God. They were heirs, but they lived like slaves in their own home.

在新約到來之前，整個世界都缺乏成熟兒女的完全權利，但他們以兩種截然不同的方式經歷了這種缺乏。對猶太人而言，他們身處神的聖約家中，卻被當作未成年人對待。保羅將摩西律法稱為 paidagogos——一個嚴格的「監護人」或紀律執行者。律法是聖潔的，但卻具有限制性。它使舊約子民處於屬靈未成熟的狀態，專注於外在的儀式、獻祭，並深刻意識到他們與神之間的距離。他們雖是繼承人，卻在自己的家中活得像奴僕。

II. The Gentile Experience: Alienation / 外邦人的經歷：與神隔絕

The Gentiles, however, were never under the "Guardian" of the Law. They were completely outside the house. They were alienated, strangers to the covenant, and enslaved to the carnal mind and pagan worldly systems. If the Jews were like children locked in a strict schoolhouse, the Gentiles were orphans wandering in the wilderness. Both groups were slaves; neither group had the standing of a mature, liberated son.

然而，外邦人從未處於律法這個「監護人」的管轄之下。他們完全被排斥在家門之外。他們與神隔絕，是諸約的局外人，受制於屬肉體的心思和異教的世俗體系。如果說猶太人就像被鎖在嚴格學校裡的孩子，那麼外邦人就是流浪在曠野中的孤兒。這兩個群體都是奴僕；兩者都不具備成熟、得自由之兒子的地位。

III. Covenantal Context: Total Victory / 約的神學背景：完全的得勝

Christ did not come to put the Gentiles under the Jewish Guardian, nor did He leave the Jews in the schoolhouse. By His finished work, culminating in the events of AD 70, He completely dissolved the Old Age. The Temple fell, the Guardian system ended, and the accuser was definitively defeated. We do not fight a present-day cosmic enemy; the victory is total. In the Eighth Day reality (the New Creation), the barrier is gone. Both Jew and Gentile step directly into the throne room as fully mature sons.

基督來，不是為了將外邦人置於猶太人的監護人之下，也不是為了讓猶太人繼續留在學校裡。藉著祂已完成的工作（並在西元 70 年的事件中達到頂峰），祂徹底終結了舊時代。聖殿倒塌了，監護人系統結束了，控告者也已被決定性地擊敗。我們今日不再與一個宇宙性的仇敵爭戰；基督的得勝是完全的。在第八日的真實（新造的現實）中，隔閡已經消除。猶太人與外邦人都能直接進入寶座前，成為完全成熟的兒子。

IV. Group Discussion / 小組討論

  1. What is the difference between obeying God out of fear of a "Guardian" versus obeying Him out of the love of a mature "Son"?
  2. 出於對「監護人」的恐懼而順服神，與出於成熟「兒子」的愛而順服神，兩者之間有何區別？
  3. Because Christ's victory over the accuser is already complete, our primary struggle today is not against an external enemy, but against our own carnal mind. Do you find your mind slipping back more into "religious rule-keeping" (the Jewish temptation) or "worldly habits" (the Gentile temptation)? How do you actively renew your mind?
  4. 既然基督對控告者的得勝已經完全，我們今日主要的掙扎便不再是抵抗外在的仇敵，而是對付我們自己屬肉體的心思。你發現自己的心思更容易退回到哪一種狀態：「宗教的規條主義」（猶太人的試探）還是「世俗的習慣」（外邦人的試探）？你如何積極地更新自己的心思？
