Lesson 1: The Two Types of Slavery

第一課：兩種奴役的狀態

"So you are no longer a slave, but a son... Formerly, when you did not know God, you were enslaved to those that by nature are not gods." (Galatians 4:7-8)

「可見，從此以後，你不是奴僕，乃是兒子了... 但從前你們不認識神的時候，是給那些本來不是神的神作奴僕。」（加拉太書 4:7-8）

"Remember that you were at that time separated from Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel and strangers to the covenants of promise, having no hope and without God in the world." (Ephesians 2:12)

「那時，你們與基督無關，在以色列國民以外，在所應許的諸約上是局外人，並且活在世上沒有指望，沒有神。」（以弗所書 2:12）

I. The Jewish Experience: Under the Guardian / 猶太人的經歷：在監護人之下

Before the New Covenant, the whole world lacked the full rights of mature sonship, but they experienced this lack in two distinct ways. For the Jews, they were inside God's family, but treated as minors. Paul calls the Mosaic Law a paidagogos—a strict "guardian" or disciplinarian. The Law was holy, but it was restrictive. It kept the Old Covenant people in a state of spiritual immaturity, focusing on outward rituals, sacrifices, and a deep awareness of their distance from God.

在新約到來之前，整個世界都缺乏成熟兒女的完全權利，但他們以兩種截然不同的方式經歷了這種缺乏。對猶太人而言，他們身處神的家中，卻被當作未成年人對待。保羅將摩西律法稱為 paidagogos——一個嚴格的「監護人」或紀律執行者。律法是聖潔的，但卻具有限制性。它使舊約子民處於屬靈未成熟的狀態，專注於外在的儀式、獻祭，並深刻意識到他們與神之間的距離。

II. The Gentile Experience: Alienation / 外邦人的經歷：與神隔絕

The Gentiles, however, were never under the "Guardian" of the Law. They were completely outside the house. They were alienated, strangers to the covenant, and enslaved to the flesh and pagan worldly systems. If the Jews were like children locked in a strict schoolhouse, the Gentiles were orphans wandering in the wilderness. Both groups were slaves; neither group had the standing of a mature, liberated son.

然而，外邦人從未處於律法這個「監護人」的管轄之下。他們完全被排斥在家門之外。他們與神隔絕，是諸約的局外人，受制於肉體和異教的世俗體系。如果說猶太人就像被鎖在嚴格學校裡的孩子，那麼外邦人就是流浪在曠野中的孤兒。這兩個群體都是奴僕；兩者都不具備成熟、得自由之兒子的地位。

III. Covenantal Context: The Eighth Day Reality / 約的神學背景：第八日的真實

Christ did not come to put the Gentiles under the Jewish Guardian, nor did He leave the Jews in the schoolhouse. By His finished work, He solved both problems. Historically, the destruction of the Temple in AD 70 marked the absolute end of the "Guardian" system. The Old Age passed away. Now, in the reality of the Eighth Day (the New Creation), the barrier is gone. Both the Jew and the Gentile are invited directly into the throne room, not as slaves, but as fully mature sons.

基督來，不是為了將外邦人置於猶太人的監護人之下，也不是為了讓猶太人繼續留在學校裡。藉著祂已完成的工作，祂同時解決了這兩個問題。在歷史上，西元 70 年聖殿的毀滅標誌著「監護人」系統的徹底終結。舊時代已經過去。現在，在第八日的真實（新造的現實）中，隔閡已經消除。猶太人與外邦人都能直接進入寶座前——不再是作為奴僕，而是作為完全成熟的兒子。

IV. Group Discussion / 小組討論

  1. What is the difference between obeying God out of fear of a "Guardian" versus obeying Him out of the love of a mature "Son"?
  2. 出於對「監護人」的恐懼而順服神，與出於成熟「兒子」的愛而順服神，兩者之間有何區別？
  3. The enemy still tries to deceive us into living like slaves. Are you more tempted to return to the slavery of "religious rule-keeping" (the Jewish temptation) or the slavery of "worldly habits" (the Gentile temptation)?
  4. 仇敵仍然試圖欺騙我們，讓我們像奴僕一樣生活。你發現自己更容易受試探回到哪一種奴役中：「宗教的規條主義」（猶太人的試探）還是「世俗的習慣」（外邦人的試探）？
