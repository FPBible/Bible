Lesson 1: The Transition from Slavery

第一課：從奴仆心態的轉變

Introduction: The journey of sonship begins by recognizing our old status. We must understand what we are moving away from in order to fully embrace the reality of the New Covenant.

引言： 兒子的旅程始於認識我們舊有的地位。我們必須清楚了解自己正脫離什麼，才能真正擁抱新約的真實。」

Scripture Reading: "For you did not receive the spirit of slavery to fall back into fear, but you have received the Spirit of adoption as sons." (Romans 8:15)

經文誦讀： 「你們所受的，不是奴僕的心，仍舊害怕；所受的，乃是兒子的心，因此我們呼叫：阿爸！父！」（羅馬書 8:15）

Discussion Point: What does "slavery" look like in our modern spiritual lives? Often, it is a performance-based mentality—serving God out of fear rather than love.

討論點： 「奴僕」心態在我們現代的屬靈生活中呈現為什麼樣？這通常表現為一種基於「表現」的心態——出於恐懼而非愛去服事神。

Reflection / 反思

1. In what area of your life do you feel you are "performing" rather than resting in your identity as a son?

1. 在你生活的哪一方面，你感覺自己是在「表演」而非安息在兒子的身份中？
