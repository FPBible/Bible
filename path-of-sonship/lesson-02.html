Lesson 2: The Origin of the Carnal Mind

第二課：屬肉體心思的起源

"But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat, for in the day that you eat of it you shall surely die." (Genesis 2:17)

「只是分別善惡樹上的果子，你不可吃，因為你吃的日子必定死！」（創世記 2:17）

"For to set the mind on the flesh is death, but to set the mind on the Spirit is life and peace." (Romans 8:6)

「體貼肉體的，就是死；體貼聖靈的，乃是生命、平安。」（羅馬書 8:6）

I. The Birth of the Performance System / 表現系統的誕生

Before the Law of Moses was given, the root of human slavery began in the Garden of Eden. Adam and Eve were offered two ways to live: The Tree of Life (representing total dependence on the Father as sons) and the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil (representing independence and self-reliance). By choosing the latter, humanity traded the rest of sonship for a system of moral accounting, judgment, and self-justification. This choice was the very birth of the carnal mind.

在摩西律法頒布之前，人類被奴役的根源始於伊甸園。亞當和夏娃面臨兩種生活方式的選擇：生命樹（代表作為兒子完全倚靠天父）以及分別善惡樹（代表獨立與自我倚靠）。藉由選擇後者，人類用兒子的安息換取了一套道德計算、審判與自我稱義的系統。這個選擇正是屬肉體心思的誕生。

II. The Universal Infection / 普世的感染

This "Knowledge of Good and Evil" became the operating system of the flesh, infecting both Gentile and Jew. For the Gentiles, it created an internal voice of constant accusation and self-defense (Romans 2:15). For the Jews, God formalized this system through the Mosaic Law, amplifying it to mathematically prove that self-justification always leads to death. Whether through worldly moral philosophy or strict religious law, the carnal mind is always obsessing over its own performance, trying to decide what is "good" and "evil" apart from God's grace.

這種「分別善惡的知識」成為了肉體的運作系統，感染了外邦人與猶太人。對外邦人而言，它在內心產生了不斷控告與自我辯護的聲音（羅馬書 2:15）。對猶太人而言，神透過摩西律法將這套系統正式化並將其放大，以精確地證明自我稱義最終必然導致死亡。無論是透過世俗的道德哲學還是嚴格的宗教律法，屬肉體的心思總是痴迷於自己的表現，試圖在神的恩典之外自行決定何為「善」、何為「惡」。

III. Covenantal Context: Return to the Tree of Life / 約的神學背景：重返生命樹

Christ did not come to help us manage our "Good and Evil" better; He came to crucify the carnal mind completely. With the end of the Old Covenant Age in AD 70, the entire formalized system of moral accounting was rendered obsolete. In the New Creation, we are brought entirely back to the Tree of Life. We no longer operate on the metric of success and failure, or performance and punishment. We operate solely on the metric of life, grace, and our finished identity as mature sons.

基督來，不是為了幫助我們更好地管理我們的「善與惡」；祂來是為了將屬肉體的心思徹底釘死在十字架上。隨著舊約時代在西元 70 年的終結，整套正式的道德計算系統已被廢棄。在新造的現實中，我們被完全帶回生命樹前。我們不再倚靠成功與失敗、表現與懲罰的標準來運作。我們單單倚靠生命、恩典，以及我們作為成熟兒子那已然成就的身份來生活。

IV. Group Discussion / 小組討論

  1. How does understanding the "Knowledge of Good and Evil" as a system of performance and self-reliance change how you view the original sin in Genesis?
  2. 將「分別善惡的知識」理解為一種追求表現和自我倚靠的系統，這如何改變你看待創世記中原罪的方式？
  3. When you feel distant from God, do you instinctively try to fix it by eating from the Tree of Knowledge (trying to do more "good" and less "evil" to earn your way back), or do you run to the Tree of Life (resting in Christ's finished work)?
  4. 當你感覺與神疏遠時，你是本能地試圖透過吃分別善惡樹的果子來彌補（試圖做更多「善」事、減少「惡」行以賺回地位），還是奔向生命樹（安息在基督已完成的工作中）？
