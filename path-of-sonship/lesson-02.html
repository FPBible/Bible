Lesson 2: The Two Trees and the Phantom Habit

第二課：兩棵樹與幻肢的習慣

"But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil you shall not eat, for in the day that you eat of it you shall surely die." (Genesis 2:17)

「只是分別善惡樹上的果子，你不可吃，因為你吃的日子必定死！」（創世記 2:17）

"Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind..." (Romans 12:2)

「不要效法這個世界，只要心意更新而變化...」（羅馬書 12:2）

I. The Root of the Carnal Mind / 屬肉體心思的根源

Before the Law of Moses existed, the root of all human slavery was planted in the Garden of Eden. Adam and Eve were presented with two opposing ways to live. The Tree of Life represented pure sonship—living in total dependence on the Father, receiving life, identity, and provision as a free gift. The Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil represented independence. By eating from it, humanity chose to define right and wrong for themselves. They traded the rest of a son for the anxiety of a judge. They embraced a system of moral accounting, measurement, and self-justification. This single choice birthed the "carnal mind" (the flesh)—an operating system obsessed with its own performance.

在摩西律法存在之前，所有人類奴役的根源已在伊甸園中種下。亞當和夏娃面臨兩種截然不同的生活方式。生命樹代表著純粹的兒子名分——完全倚靠天父而活，將生命、身份與供應視為白白領受的恩典。而分別善惡樹則代表著獨立。藉由吃這樹上的果子，人類選擇了自行定義對錯。他們用兒子的安息換取了法官的焦慮。他們接受了一套道德計算、衡量與自我稱義的系統。這單一的選擇孕育了「屬肉體的心思」（肉體）——一個痴迷於自身表現的運作系統。

II. The Exhaustion of the Old Covenant / 舊約的耗盡

For centuries, humanity lived under the shadow of this Tree. For the Gentiles, it was an internal voice of constant accusation and self-defense. For the Jews, God formalized this system through the Mosaic Law (the Guardian). God gave the Law not to save them, but to mathematically prove that the Tree of Knowledge always leads to death. A system based on human performance, striving, and measuring "good vs. evil" is exhausting and impossible to satisfy. The Old Covenant Temple, with its endless sacrifices and strict boundaries, was the ultimate monument to the fact that the carnal mind can never truly justify itself or achieve lasting peace with God.

幾個世紀以來，人類都活在這棵樹的陰影下。對外邦人而言，它是內心不斷控告與自我辯護的聲音。對猶太人而言，神透過摩西律法（監護人）將這套系統正式化。神頒布律法不是為了拯救他們，而是為了精確地證明：分別善惡樹最終必然導致死亡。一個建立在人類表現、努力與衡量「善與惡」之上的系統是令人筋疲力盡且永遠無法滿足的。舊約的聖殿及其無止盡的祭祀與嚴格的界線，正是最極致的紀念碑，證明了屬肉體的心思永遠無法真正稱義，也無法與神建立持久的和平。

III. The Covenantal Reality: A Dead Tree / 約的神學現實：一棵死樹

Christ did not come to help us perform better under the Tree of Knowledge; He came to cut the tree down. Through His finished work on the cross and the final destruction of the Old Covenant system in AD 70, the age of moral accounting was legally and completely abolished. In the courtroom of heaven, the metric of "Good and Evil" as a means of justification has been permanently thrown out. We cannot undo Christ's finished work. Covenantally, the Tree of Knowledge is dead. In the reality of the Eighth Day (the New Creation), our unchangeable, permanent status is that of mature sons, justified entirely by grace.

基督來，不是為了幫助我們在分別善惡樹下表現得更好；祂來是為了砍倒這棵樹。透過祂在十字架上已完成的工作，以及西元 70 年舊約系統的最終毀滅，這個道德計算的時代在律法上被徹底廢除了。在天堂的法庭上，將「善與惡」作為稱義標準的度量衡已被永久拋棄。我們無法推翻基督已完成的工作。在聖約上，分別善惡樹已經死了。在第八日的真實（新造的現實）中，我們擁有不可改變、永久的地位，那就是完全本乎恩典稱義的成熟兒子。

IV. The Experiential Reality: The Phantom Habit / 經歷上的現實：幻肢的習慣

If the old system is completely destroyed, why do believers still struggle with guilt, fear, and the desire to "earn" God's love? The answer lies in understanding the "Phantom Limb." When a person has a limb amputated, they often still feel pain or itching in the arm that is no longer there. The limb is gone, but the neurological habit remains in the brain. Covenantally, the system of performance has been amputated. Yet, our unrenewed carnal minds still experience "phantom habits." When we try to earn God's approval through religious duties, or when we fall into despair because we didn't perform well enough, we are not returning to the Old Covenant. We are simply reverting to a dead habit. We are trying to pick fruit from a tree that no longer exists.

如果舊系統已經被徹底摧毀，為什麼信徒仍然會與內疚、恐懼以及想要「賺取」神之愛的渴望爭戰？答案在於理解「幻肢」的現象。當一個人被截肢後，他們通常仍會在失去的手臂上感到疼痛或發癢。肢體已經不在了，但神經的習慣依然留在腦海中。在聖約上，追求表現的系統已被截除。然而，我們未經更新的屬肉體心思仍會經歷「幻肢的習慣」。當我們試圖透過宗教義務來賺取神的認可，或當我們因為表現不佳而陷入絕望時，我們並沒有回到舊約。我們只是退回到一個死寂的習慣中。我們正試圖從一棵已不存在的樹上摘取果子。

V. Group Discussion / 小組討論

  1. Look at your own prayer life and daily walk. In what specific ways do you catch yourself treating God like an "accountant" (measuring your good and bad deeds) rather than a "Father"?
  2. 審視你自己的禱告生活和日常行事。在哪些具體方面，你發現自己將神視為一名「會計師」（衡量你的善行與惡行），而不是一位「父親」？
  3. Understanding the "Phantom Limb" means realizing your feelings of condemnation have no legal standing in heaven. How can this truth help you renew your mind the next time you feel you have failed spiritually?
  4. 理解「幻肢」意味著要明白，你內心那些被定罪的感覺在天堂裡是沒有任何法律效力的。當你下次在屬靈上感到挫敗時，這個真理可以如何幫助你更新你的心思？
