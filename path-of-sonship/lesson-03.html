Lesson 3: The Dividing Wall Destroyed

第三課：拆毀隔斷的牆

"For he himself is our peace, who has made us both one and has broken down in his flesh the dividing wall of hostility... that he might create in himself one new man in place of the two, so making peace." (Ephesians 2:14-15)

「因他使我們和睦，將兩下合而為一，拆毀了中間隔斷的牆... 為要將兩下藉著自己造成一個新人，如此便成就了和睦。」（以弗所書 2:14-15）

"There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither slave nor free, there is no male and female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus." (Galatians 3:28)

「並不分猶太人、希臘人，自主的、為奴的，或男或女，因為你們在基督耶穌裡都成為一了。」（加拉太書 3:28）

I. The Physical Boundary / 實體的界線

In the first two lessons, we saw that the whole world was enslaved: the Jews under the rigid "Guardian" of the Law, and the Gentiles alienated in the wilderness of the carnal mind. This spiritual separation was visibly mirrored in the physical world. In the Jerusalem Temple, there was a literal stone barricade called the Soreg. It separated the inner courts (where Jews could worship) from the outer court (the Court of the Gentiles). Signs were posted on this wall warning any Gentile who crossed it that they would be responsible for their own ensuing death. This wall was the ultimate symbol of the Old Covenant system: division, exclusion, and a performance-based proximity to God.

在前兩課中，我們看到整個世界都處於奴役之中：猶太人在嚴格的律法「監護人」之下，而外邦人則在屬肉體心思的曠野中與神隔絕。這種屬靈上的分隔，在物質世界上也有著具體的對應。在耶路撒冷的聖殿中，有一道真實的石牆稱為「梭烈格」（Soreg）。它將內院（猶太人敬拜的地方）與外院（外邦人院）隔開。牆上貼有警告標語，任何越過此牆的外邦人都必須為自己隨之而來的死亡負責。這道牆是舊約系統最極致的象徵：分裂、排斥，以及基於表現來決定與神親近的程度。

II. The Creation of One New Man / 一個新人的創造

When Christ came, He did not bring the Gentiles into the inner court to place them under the Jewish Guardian. Nor did He send the Jews out into the Gentile wilderness. Instead, He accomplished something completely radical: He destroyed the wall entirely to create a brand-new humanity. By fulfilling the Law, He ended the system that required the separation. In Christ, the two former slaves are not simply merged; they are entirely transformed into "One New Man." They are no longer measured by their fleshly background, but by their new, shared identity as mature sons.

當基督降臨時，祂並沒有將外邦人帶入內院，讓他們受猶太監護人的管轄。祂也沒有將猶太人趕到外邦人的曠野中。相反，祂完成了一件極具顛覆性的事：祂徹底拆毀了那道牆，創造了一個全新的族類。藉著成全律法，祂終結了那個需要隔離的系統。在基督裡，這兩個曾經的奴僕並非只是被合併在一起；他們被完全轉化為「一個新人」。他們不再根據肉體的背景來被衡量，而是根據他們作為成熟兒女那嶄新且共享的身份。

III. Covenantal Context: The End of Exclusion / 約的神學背景：排斥的終結

The spiritual destruction of this dividing wall was established at the cross, but it was historically and permanently finalized in AD 70. When the Roman army dismantled the physical Temple, stone by stone, the "dividing wall of hostility" was wiped off the face of the earth. The Old Age of exclusion was objectively ended. In the reality of the Eighth Day, there is no "inner court" for the elite and "outer court" for the unworthy. The veil is torn. The wall is crushed. The throne room is open, and every believer possesses equal, unrestricted access to the Father.

這道隔斷的牆在屬靈上的毀滅是建立在十字架上的，但在歷史上，它是在西元 70 年被永久且徹底地終結的。當羅馬軍隊將那座實體的聖殿一磚一瓦地拆毀時，那道「隔斷的牆」便從地球上被抹去了。排斥的舊時代客觀地結束了。在第八日的真實中，不再有專屬精英的「內院」，也不再有留給不配之人的「外院」。幔子已經裂開，牆垣已被粉碎。寶座前的門是敞開的，每一個信徒都擁有平等、不受限制地親近天父的權利。

IV. The Experiential Reality: Dropping Our Stones / 經歷上的現實：放下我們的石頭

Even though the wall is legally and historically destroyed, our unrenewed carnal minds (the "phantom habit") still love to build walls. The flesh instinctively categorizes people into "us" and "them" to feel superior or secure. We construct invisible walls in our churches based on theology, behavior, or background. We try to recreate the Old Covenant system of exclusion. But as mature sons of the Eighth Day, we are called to rest in the reality that the wall is already gone, and to stop trying to rebuild what Christ has torn down.

儘管這道牆在律法與歷史上已被拆毀，我們未經更新的屬肉體心思（「幻肢的習慣」）仍然喜歡築牆。肉體會本能地將人歸類為「我們」和「他們」，以獲得優越感或安全感。我們在教會中基於神學、行為或背景築起無形的牆。我們試圖重建舊約那套排斥的系統。但作為第八日的成熟兒女，我們被呼召去安息在這道牆已經消失的現實中，並停止試圖重建基督已經拆毀的東西。

V. Group Discussion / 小組討論

  1. The destruction of the physical Temple in AD 70 means God no longer has a centralized, physical "holy place" that excludes certain people. How does this New Creation reality change the way you view the people sitting around you right now?
  2. 西元 70 年實體聖殿的毀滅，意味著神不再擁有一個會排斥某些人的集中化、實體的「聖地」。這個新造的現實如何改變你看待此刻坐在你周圍的人的方式？
  3. Because of our "phantom habits," we often rebuild invisible walls. What is an invisible "dividing wall" you have caught yourself building between you and other believers in the past, and how does Christ's finished work call you to tear it down?
  4. 因為我們的「幻肢習慣」，我們經常會重建無形的牆。過去你曾發現自己在你與其他信徒之間築起了什麼樣無形的「隔斷的牆」？基督已完成的工作如何呼召你將其拆毀？
