Curriculum Overview / 課程大綱

This 24-session curriculum is designed for small groups, home fellowships, and personal study. It explores the transition from the Old Covenant to the New, focusing on the reality of our identity in Christ.

這套 24 課的課程專為小組聚會、家庭團契及個人查經而設計。課程深入探討從舊約向新約的過渡，專注於我們在基督裡的新造身份。
