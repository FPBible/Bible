What this project is

FPBible is a reading edition of the World English Bible (WEB) with chapter-level commentary shaped by three commitments: audience relevance, the AD 30–70 covenant transition, and the New Creation as present reality.

The goal is not a new denomination or a debate club. It is a clear, scripture-first path for readers who want the time statements of Jesus and the apostles taken at face value inside the first-century horizon.

How to read

Audience relevance

Every time statement, warning, and promise is read first for the people who first heard it — the generation Jesus addressed.

Time statements

“This generation,” “soon,” “at hand,” and related language are not emptied into a remote unknown. They mark the closing of the Old Covenant age.

New Creation as present

What cannot be shaken remains. The unshakable Kingdom is the ground under the feet of those in Christ — not a postponed world after history ends.

What it is / isn’t

It is: a fulfilled-prophecy reading of Scripture that treats AD 70 as the historical close of the old order and the open reality of the New Covenant “one new man.”

It is not: a denial of physical death, a claim that every personal struggle is finished, or hostility toward Jewish heritage. The judgment in view is covenantal and historical — the end of the Temple administration — not a license for contempt.
