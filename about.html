About FPBible

FPBible is a reader-first Bible library using the World English Bible (WEB) as base text, with commentary anchored in the fulfilled covenant-transition horizon (AD 30–70) and the lived reality of the New Creation.

Start Reading

What this project is

The Fulfilled Prophecy Bible presents Scripture with notes and commentary that honour audience relevance and time statements. Warnings, promises, and “coming” language are read first for the generation that heard them — especially the transition from the Old Covenant order to the New Covenant reality completed in the events culminating in AD 70.

The goal is not a new translation, but a consistent reading path: WEB text, clear structure, and commentary that refuses to push first-century fulfillment into an endless future.

How to read

Audience relevance

Ask first: who first heard these words, and what did they mean in that covenant setting?

Time statements

“This generation,” “soon,” “at hand,” and similar language are treated as binding for the original audience unless the text itself expands the horizon.

New Creation as present

After the cross, the Spirit, and the end of the old order, believers live in the New Covenant age — the “Eighth Day” reality of one new man in Christ.

What it is / isn’t

  • Is: WEB Scripture with fulfillment-focused commentary and Prophecy Hub links.
  • Is: Oriented to AD 30–70 as the decisive covenant transition.
  • Is not: Delay theology or speculative futurism as the default frame.
  • Is not: A claim that physical mortality has been abolished; physical death remains, but its covenantal sting is dealt with in Christ.
