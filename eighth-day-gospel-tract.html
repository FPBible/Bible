The Eighth Day Has Dawned

In the year 70 the old world ended. The temple fell. The age of shadows closed. What many still treat as future has already happened. The judgment came. The dividing wall is gone.

AD 70 — The end of the age

The Old Order

Under the Old Covenant, identity was genealogy. Access was limited. The law stood as a tutor, and the temple as a barrier. Humanity lived under the shadow of Adam’s fall — not because physical death was unnatural, but because covenantal death and alienation had entered the picture.

Genealogy. Law. Temple. Distance.

The Last Adam

Into that old order came the Last Adam. Jesus did what the first Adam could not. He lived in unbroken dependence on the Father. At the cross He bore the covenantal weight — the condemnation and alienation of the old creation. The resurrection was the beginning of the New Creation.

The Last Adam — New Creation begun

The Generation of Fulfillment

Jesus told His own generation that the end of the age would come upon them. Between the cross and the fall of the temple, the transition unfolded. In AD 70 the old system was removed. The age of types and shadows ended.

AD 30–70 — The wilderness of transition

One New Man

With the old covenant finished, the dividing wall of hostility is gone. There is no longer Jew or Gentile as covenant categories that separate. There is one new man in Christ. Identity is no longer genealogy or DNA. It is faith. Sonship has replaced slavery.

One new man · No dividing wall · Sons, not slaves
