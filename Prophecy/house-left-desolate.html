Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Your House Left Desolate

Not “a house” — your house. Ownership is handed back to the tenants He is leaving.

Plain

Ezekiel watched glory depart the first temple before it fell. Jesus’ sentence is that departure spoken in advance of the second.

In Matthew

Matthew 23 ends the woes with the desolate house. Matthew 24 opens with the disciples pointing at the buildings He just emptied of meaning.

First-Century Horizon

Desolation became architecture in AD 70. The sentence was not withdrawn.

Scripture

  • Matthew 23:37–39
  • Jeremiah 22:5
  • Ezekiel 10

New Creation Reality

The desolate house is not your house. The filled house is the people with Immanuel.
