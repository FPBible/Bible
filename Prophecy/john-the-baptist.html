Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

John the Baptist

If you misplace John, you will misplace the Day he announced.

Plain

He stands in the wilderness with axe and winnowing fork, not with a millennia-long rain check.

In Matthew

Matthew opens public ministry with John, then lets Jesus interpret John as Elijah and as more than a prophet.

First-Century Horizon

John’s generation is the generation that saw both the Lamb and the fall of the house he warned.

Scripture

  • Isaiah 40:3–5
  • Malachi 3:1
  • Matthew 3:1–12; Matthew 11:7–15

New Creation Reality

The forerunner has finished his course. The Lord he named is the Lord of the New Creation.
