Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

New Wine / New Covenant

You cannot pour the Messiah’s life into Sinai’s wineskin and keep both intact.

Plain

The prophets promised new things — new heart, new covenant, new song. Jesus says the wedding party has started.

In Matthew

Matthew 9’s skins sit next to a new kind of forgiveness and a new kind of table-fellowship.

First-Century Horizon

The old skin tore in that generation. Trying to stitch it back on is not faithfulness to Moses. It is refusal of the feast.

Scripture

  • Matthew 9:14–17
  • Jeremiah 31:31–34
  • Luke 22:20

New Creation Reality

The wine is new. The skin is the New Covenant people.
