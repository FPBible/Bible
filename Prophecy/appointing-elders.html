Appointing Elders

Fulfilled-prophecy study on “Appointing Elders” within the AD 30–70 covenant transition.

In the First-Century Horizon

Paul and Barnabas appoint elders in every church (Acts 14:23). Care of the flock is ordered while the mission advances and the old age closes.

Scripture’s Claim

Eldership is not a delayed institution; it belongs to the apostolic planting of churches in that generation.

New Creation Reality

The word of grace builds up; shepherds guard what was purchased with blood.
