Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Isaiah 53 Fulfillment

He took our infirmities is Matthew’s caption over healings, not over a later political Israel.

Plain

Isaiah’s Servant is despised, pierced, and makes many righteous. The Gospels put that life on one Man.

In Matthew

Matthew 8 quotes Isaiah 53 over sick bodies. Matthew 20 and 26 put the same Servant on the road to a ransom-death.

First-Century Horizon

The apostolic generation preached a Servant already exalted. AD 70 did not create that fulfillment; it exposed those who refused it.

Scripture

  • Isaiah 53:1–12
  • Matthew 8:16–17; Matthew 20:28
  • 1 Peter 2:21–25

New Creation Reality

The Servant has finished the work. New Creation healing is participation in that finished wound, not a wait for another Servant.
