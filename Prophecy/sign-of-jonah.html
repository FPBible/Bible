Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

The Sign of Jonah

Jonah spent three days in the deep and then preached overthrow to a city that actually repented. Jesus claims that pattern.

Plain

The sign is the prophet’s descent and emergence, then a word of judgment. Nineveh heard. “This generation” mostly did not.

In Matthew

Matthew 12 and 16 refuse extra credentials. Resurrection is the credential.

First-Century Horizon

Jonah’s city turned. Jerusalem, with a greater than Jonah, doubled down until the overthrow arrived.

Scripture

  • Jonah 1–3
  • Matthew 12:38–41; 16:4
  • Matthew 27:63–64; 28:1–10

New Creation Reality

The sign has been given. New Creation faith does not wait for a different one.
