Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Law and Prophets Fulfilled

Fulfill means fill full — arrive at the intended end — not erase and postpone.

Plain

The Law was a tutor and a covenant administration. The Prophets aimed at a coming day. Both terminate in the Messiah.

In Matthew

Matthew 5:17–18 sits next to “heaven and earth passing.” Matthew 17 puts Moses and Elijah on the mountain and then leaves Jesus alone.

First-Century Horizon

When the temple fell, the public system that had administered Moses as a standing world-order was removed. The fulfillment was already the Son.

Scripture

  • Matthew 5:17–18; Matthew 17:1–8
  • Romans 10:4
  • Hebrews 8:13

New Creation Reality

You are not under a tutor waiting for a later graduation. The goal has a name.
