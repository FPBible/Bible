Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Gentile Inclusion

Gentile inclusion is Abraham’s blessing arriving on schedule — not Plan B after Israel fails a future test.

Plain

The dividing wall of the Law marked Jew and Gentile in the old administration. In the Messiah that wall is announced as finished. Matthew shows the opening in narrative before Paul explains it in letters.

In Matthew

Magi worship. A Roman centurion is said to have greater faith than anyone in Israel. A Canaanite woman is given bread. The risen Christ sends disciples to all nations.

First-Century Horizon

By the time the temple fell, mixed congregations already existed across the empire. AD 70 did not start inclusion. It removed the institution that had been used to deny it.

Scripture

  • Matthew 2:1–12; 8:5–13; 15:21–28; 28:18–20
  • Isaiah 49:6
  • Ephesians 2:11–22

New Creation Reality

The nations are not guests at Abraham’s table. In Christ they are family.
