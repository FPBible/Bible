The Yoke Neither Our Fathers Nor We Could Bear

Peter names the Law as a yoke the nation could not carry; salvation is through the grace of the Lord Jesus.

Acts 15:10–11

Why test God by placing a yoke on the disciples? We believe we are saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus, in the same way as they.

End of the Administration

The yoke belongs to the old covenant order that was passing away in the apostolic generation.

Grace Alone

Jew and Gentile alike stand by grace, not by the works of the Law.
