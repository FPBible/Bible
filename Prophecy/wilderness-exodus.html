Out of the old house. The gate is still ahead.

Israel was brought out before she was brought in. Deliverance at the sea was not the land. A generation lived between those two doors.

That shape sits over the years from the cross to AD 70. The old house is behind them. The possession is not yet public. What lies between is wilderness — and wilderness is not where the story ends.

The Sea

They leave the house of bondage. The door out is behind them.

The Wilderness

One generation. The old tent still standing. Hearts exposed.

The River

A crossing. The old generation does not take the land.

The map is enough for this page. The crossing is the book.

The Interval

Between Ascension and the public end of the old house, Christ reigns. That reign is not an empty wait. It is the wilderness of a people already delivered and not yet settled.

Bread in the wilderness was never a second Egypt. It was provision for a generation on the way. The same question meets the transition generation: will they live on the bread of the house they have left, or follow the One who is leading them in?

The harvest calendar names the same interval from sheaf to ingathering. This book names it from the river.

Crossing the Jordan

Crossing the Jordan is the next book. It walks the shadow the article only opens: why the first Exodus took a generation to bury the old house, and why the new Exodus did the same.

  • Who is already out.
  • Who does not cross.
  • What the land actually is, once the river is behind.

The page stops at the bank. The book takes the crossing.
