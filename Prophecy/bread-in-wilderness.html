Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Bread in the Wilderness

The crowd eats in a deserted place because the true bread has arrived.

Plain

Manna taught dependence. Jesus multiplies loaves and then names Himself as the point of the sign.

In Matthew

Matthew 14 and 15 put Gentile and Jewish crowds at the same kind of table. The wilderness is no longer a Jew-only camp.

First-Century Horizon

When the old house fell, the people already knew where bread was found.

Scripture

  • Exodus 16:4–15
  • Matthew 14:13–21; Matthew 15:32–39
  • John 6:31–35

New Creation Reality

You are not waiting for another manna season. The bread of the New Creation is the Son.
