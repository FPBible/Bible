Wheat and Chaff Separation

Chaff is the husk of a harvest being cleared — not a label for every unbeliever until the end of matter.

Plain

Threshing floors in the prophets are judgment scenes. John says the Coming One already has the fork in His hand — present tense.

In Matthew

Matthew 3’s wheat and chaff prepares Matthew 13’s wheat and weeds. Both are about a field God is finishing in that age.

First-Century Horizon

The remnant gathered into the barn is the New Covenant people. The chaff of the old administration is cleared when that world ends.

Scripture

  • Psalm 1:4; Malachi 4:1
  • Matthew 3:12; 13:24–30, 36–43

New Creation Reality

You are not living in the winnowing. You are living as wheat already stored in the King’s house.
