Binding and Releasing

Binding and loosing is rabbinic legal speech — forbid and permit — not a magic keychain.

Plain

Isaiah’s key of David opens and shuts. Jesus gives that key-work to a people who confess Him.

In Matthew

Matthew 16 and 18 attach the keys to confession of the Son and to church discipline inside that generation’s assembly.

First-Century Horizon

The old Sanhedrin claimed the keys of Moses. The Messiah relocates the court to those who receive Him before the old court falls.

New Creation Reality

The New Creation people announce an already-decided verdict: open in the Son, shut outside Him.
