Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Light to the Nations

Galilee of the nations is not a detour. It is the map of the promise.

Plain

Isaiah saw a great light in Zebulun and Naphtali, and a servant who would not stop until the coastlands waited for His law.

In Matthew

Matthew quotes Isaiah 9 when Jesus settles in Capernaum. The light starts in despised territory and runs outward. Chapter 28 is that light finishing its road.

First-Century Horizon

Paul later says he is sent as a light to the Gentiles. That is the same Isaiah program in the same generation.

Scripture

  • Isaiah 9:1–2; 42:6; 49:6
  • Matthew 4:12–17; 28:18–20
  • Acts 13:47

New Creation Reality

The nations walk in a light that has already risen.
