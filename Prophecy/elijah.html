Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Elijah Fulfilled

If Elijah already came, the Day he introduced is not an unused ticket.

Plain

Malachi’s last page is not a modern biography assignment. It is a covenant warning attached to a messenger.

In Matthew

Matthew 11 and 17 treat John as Elijah. The disciples want a later Elijah; Jesus says the role landed.

First-Century Horizon

John prepared a people. That people saw the Day in their generation. Looking for a second Elijah to restart Malachi empties Jesus’ sentence.

Scripture

  • Malachi 4:5–6
  • Matthew 11:14; Matthew 17:10–13
  • Luke 1:16–17

New Creation Reality

The messenger came. The Lord he announced is the One you walk with now.
