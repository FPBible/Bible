Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Authority of Christ

Daniel 7’s dominion is here named as present possession. Nothing is reserved for a later coronation.

Plain

Authority in heaven and earth is total world-order language. It is what the risen Son holds before the disciples leave the mountain.

In Matthew

Matthew began with a king-genealogy and ends with a king-commission. Baptising nations and teaching His commands is how that authority runs in public.

First-Century Horizon

A church that preaches a King who does not yet have authority is not preaching Matthew’s last page.

Scripture

  • Matthew 28:16–20
  • Daniel 7:13–14
  • Matthew 11:27

New Creation Reality

The authority is His. New Creation discipleship is obedience under a seated King.
