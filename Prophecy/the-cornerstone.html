The Rejected Stone: Psalm 118 and the New Temple

The stone the builders rejected becomes the head of the corner — Christ as foundation of the true temple.

Psalm 118 Applied

Peter tells the Sanhedrin that Jesus is the stone regarded as worthless by the builders, which has become the head of the corner (Acts 4:11; Psalm 118:22).

Builders Displaced

The builders were the leaders responsible for God’s house. By rejecting the cornerstone they disqualified themselves; the New Temple is built on Christ and the apostles.

Unshaken After AD 70

When the stone temple fell, the cornerstone remained. The house built on Him could not be demolished with Jerusalem.
