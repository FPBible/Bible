The Cornerstone

Jesus the rejected stone becomes the cornerstone of a new temple — the people of God, not a building of graded courts.

Psalm 118 and the Apostolic Claim

“The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone” (Psalm 118:22). Peter applies this directly to Jesus before the rulers in Jerusalem (Acts 4:11). The builders — the leadership of the old house — rejected Him; God made Him the head of the corner.

Isaiah’s tested stone in Zion (Isaiah 28:16) and the stumbling stone (Isaiah 8:14) converge in the same Christ: precious to believers, a rock of offense to those who refuse the transition.

Cornerstone of What?

Not of a renovated Herodian complex. Paul says the household of God is built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus Himself being the cornerstone, in whom the whole structure grows into a holy temple in the Lord (Ephesians 2:20–22). The temple is the people. The Spirit indwells a living building.

First Peter 2 echoes the same: living stones, a spiritual house, a holy priesthood — those who believe will not be put to shame, while the disobedient stumble over the word as they were appointed to do in that crisis of the ages.

Builders and Judgment

Jesus’ parable of the vineyard ends with the rejected son and the transfer of the vineyard (Matthew 21:33–46). The chief priests and Pharisees perceive He speaks of them. The stone that crushes is not a random catastrophe; it is the judgment that falls on the administration that refused the cornerstone.

Rejection by the builders; exaltation by God; judgment on the house that refused Him.
That sequence belongs to the first-century horizon culminating in AD 70 — not to an endless cycle of delay.

New Creation Reality

The church does not wait for a future cornerstone event. The stone is already laid. Life in the New Covenant is life in the temple He is building — aligned to Christ, not to the rejected floor plan of the old courts.

Read: Psalm 118; Isaiah 28:16; Acts 4:11; Ephesians 2:19–22; 1 Peter 2:4–8; Matthew 21:42–45 (WEB)
