Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

Outer Darkness

The shock in Matthew 8 is that sons of the kingdom are thrown out while nations recline with Abraham.

Plain

Darkness outside a lit feast is covenant exclusion. Gnashing of teeth is rage and grief, not a geology of the underworld.

In Matthew

Matthew uses the cluster for people who assumed pedigree guaranteed a seat. Matthew 22 and 25 reuse it for wedding guests and worthless servants.

First-Century Horizon

Those who claimed the table by flesh watched Gentiles take the seats while their city went dark.

Scripture

  • Matthew 8:11–12; 22:13; 25:30
  • Psalm 112:10
  • Luke 13:28–29

New Creation Reality

The feast is the New Covenant table. The darkness was the exile of an order that refused the Host.
