Covenant Lawsuit and Woes

The woes are not generic insults. They are a legal brief against that generation’s stewards.

Plain

The prophets sued Israel with “woe,” vineyard, and blood-guilt. Jesus files the last brief in the temple courts.

In Matthew

Matthew 23 names tombs, strain-out-gnats, and a desolate house, then timestamps it: this generation.

First-Century Horizon

AD 70 is the published verdict of a case already argued in the woes.

Scripture

  • Matthew 23:1–39
  • Deuteronomy 32:15–43
  • Isaiah 5:1–7

New Creation Reality

The lawsuit is closed. New Creation life is life after the sentence, not life waiting for a retrial.
