Bible Prophecy Hub Matthew

The Day of Judgment

Cities of Jesus’ own circuit are told they will fare worse than Tyre and Sodom on that Day.

Plain

The prophets’ Day of the Lord lands on cities in history. Jesus uses that same court calendar.

In Matthew

Matthew 10–12 and 11:20–24 aim the Day at Chorazin, Bethsaida, and Capernaum, not at a nameless later planet.

First-Century Horizon

When Jerusalem fell, the Day those towns were warned about had arrived in public.

Scripture

  • Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:20–24; Matthew 12:36–42
  • Isaiah 13:6–13
  • Malachi 4:1–3

New Creation Reality
