Pentecost and the Spirit

The Spirit poured out at Pentecost inaugurates the last days of the Old Covenant age.

Joel Fulfilled in 'These Days'

Peter announces that the Pentecost outpouring is what Joel spoke concerning the last days (Acts 2:16–17). Those last days are the closing period of the Old Covenant order, bounded by the generation that saw the cross, the Spirit, and the fall of the Temple.

Presence Relocated

Tongues of fire and the sound of wind signal Sinai’s God now resting on a people. The Temple made with hands is no longer the exclusive locus of glory; the living temple has begun.

Until the Day of the Lord

Joel’s cosmic signs frame the Day of the Lord that ended that age in AD 70. Pentecost starts the transition; judgment on the old house completes it.
