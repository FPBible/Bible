Across the Jordan

Leaving the Wilderness of Fear and Living in the Promised Land of the New Creation

If you have ever lived with a low hum of dread, scanning the horizon for the next catastrophe, this book is for you.

Across the Jordan follows the book of Joshua through the crossing and the first days in the land. It shows how the forty years between the cross and AD 70 formed the church’s wilderness generation. When the old order was removed, God’s people crossed over. We are not waiting on the far side of the river. We are standing in the land under the greater Joshua.

Core Themes

  • Moses dies, Joshua leads: The Law can bring you to the edge. Only Yeshua can bring you in.
  • Manna ceases, fruit begins: From temporary signs to permanent Word and fruit of the Spirit.
  • Gilgal: The reproach of Egypt rolled away. Identity as heir, not recovering slave.
  • Strongholds fall: The real walls are in the mind. The Commander has already won.
  • Tabernacle at Shiloh: God’s dwelling is no longer a building. It is His people.
  • Sabbath rest: The work is finished. We live from rest, not toward it.
The Jordan is behind you. The land is beneath your feet. The Presence is within you. And the rest has begun.
