The Feeling

Something Is Off

Most people feel it. A distance that never quite closes. The sense that we are always striving and never quite arriving. Divisions that keep reappearing no matter how hard we try to fix them.

Religion tries to manage it with rules and rituals. Irreligion tries to manage it by ignoring it. Neither ends the problem.

Something deeper is unfinished.

One Man

In the first century a man named Jesus appeared. He taught, healed, and was executed. His followers claimed he was raised.

He did not offer a better set of rules. He claimed the old way of relating to God was ending, and a new way was beginning.

Not by human effort. By something he himself would finish.

Distance by Design

Jesus lived inside a system built on ancestry and performance. Who you were born as decided your standing. A temple stood as the center, and most people were kept at a distance from it.

The system was not accidental. It was the old order. Jesus said that order would end in the lifetime of the people listening to him.

The dividing categories would not last.

The End of the Old Order

In the year 70 the temple was destroyed. The entire system collapsed. The wall that separated people by birth and status came down.

What many still treat as future had already occurred. The age of shadows closed. The old way of distance and striving ended.

One New Humanity

There is now one new humanity. Identity is no longer genealogy or effort. It is trust in the one who finished the old order.

Access that was once restricted is open. The dividing categories are gone. You do not become acceptable by becoming better. You enter by faith into what has already been accomplished.

The Door Is Open

The old system of distance is finished. The work is done. You are invited to leave the old identity—whatever it was—and take your place in the new humanity by simple trust.

The presence that was once closed is open.
The Eighth Day has dawned.
