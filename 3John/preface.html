Preface — How to Read 3 John

3 John is the most personal of the three short letters. The elder writes to a man he loves in truth — Gaius — and holds him up as an example of walking in truth and supporting those who go out for the sake of the Name.

The letter also names the opposite spirit: Diotrephes, who loves to be first, refuses the brothers, and throws out of the assembly those who would receive them.

What this letter shows

  • Walking in truth is the source of the elder’s greatest joy.
  • Supporting those who go out for the Name makes us fellow workers for the truth.
  • Self-promotion and control that reject the brothers stand in direct opposition to the life of the New Covenant community.
  • Imitate what is good; the one who does good is of God.

How to read it

Read 3 John as a snapshot of real life in the assemblies during the closing decades of the old covenant age. Truth is already present. Love is already being practised by some. Opposition is already active. The call is clear: continue walking in the truth and become fellow workers with those who go out for the Name.
