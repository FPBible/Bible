3 John

Fellow Workers for the Truth · Walk in Truth

This short letter from the elder to beloved Gaius celebrates a man who walks in truth and supports those who go out for the sake of the Name. It also confronts the self-promoting opposition of Diotrephes, who refuses the brothers and throws out those who would receive them.

The letter shows the practical shape of New Covenant life: truth lived, love expressed in support of the work, and clear refusal of self-seeking control.
