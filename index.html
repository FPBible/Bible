Start Here

Core entry points to establish your foundation

Step 1: Ground Your Identity

  • • If you are new to faith or church language, read What is the Good News?
  • • If you are recovering from performance-based religion, read From Slave to Mature Heir.
  • • For the full structured journey into fulfilled prophecy and present sonship, begin with The Eighth Day Reality Curriculum.
  • • To map out the biblical mechanics of the transition, read The Covenantal Framework of Salvation.

Step 2: Explore the Foundations

  1. Read the New Creation manifesto (2 minutes) — presence and settled life.
  2. Read the Resurrection page (5–10 minutes) — life now in Christ.
  3. Read The End of the Law — covenant jurisdiction in Christ.
  4. Read The Day of Atonement — Hebrews 9 anchor (appearing / judgment / salvation).

Then:

  • Open a finished book index and read chapter-by-chapter.
  • Use "Scripture (WEB)" links in chapters to verify the base text.
  • Use the Prophecy Hub when you hit “coming / wrath / day / fire / resurrection / heaven & earth”.
