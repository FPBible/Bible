How to read

Audience relevance

Every time statement, warning, and promise is read first for the people who first heard it — the generation Jesus addressed.

AD 30–70 horizon

The cross, the Spirit, and the fall of the Temple close the Old Covenant age and open the New Creation reality we live in now.

Connecting Scripture

The New Testament does not invent its language of clouds, enthronement, desolation, and unshakable kingdom. It quotes the Psalms — and those psalms establish the Hebrew idioms for the AD 70 covenant transition and the New Creation.

Psalms — AD 70 Patterns

Eight studies: cosmic “coming,” enthronement of the Son, suffering and old Zion’s desolation, shaking and the garment of the old order.

  • Psalm 18 · 97–98 — clouds & judgment
  • Psalm 2 · 110 — enthronement
  • Psalm 22 · 69 — cross & desolation
  • Psalm 46 · 102 — shaking & permanence

Why this matters

Jesus and the apostles spoke with Israel’s songbook. “Coming on clouds,” enemies as a footstool, house left desolate, heavens worn like a garment — these are covenant words first, not newspaper end-of-the-world scripts.

Read the OT patterns, then the NT fulfillment notes in the finished books. One story: Old Covenant end, New Creation present.
