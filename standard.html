The Fulfilled Prophecy Bible — Merged Master Standard

SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH (LOCKED FOR HUMAN & AI BUILD PIPELINES)

This standard merges and replaces all previous versions of standard.html and _AI_PROMPT.txt. Every chapter page in the repository must adhere strictly to this locked structure.

Reconciled Standard Rules: 10-part structural order locked. Commentary block locked as Fulfillment Commentary with a strict 500+ word floor. Reader-facing pages must assert meaning directly without meta-methodology. Non-dualistic Hebrew monotheism locked across all demonic and exorcism references.

1) LOCKED 10-PART CHAPTER PAGE SEQUENCE

Every chapter page must render the following 10 sections in this exact order:

  1. Immediate Context: Single direct paragraph stating speaker, audience, covenant moment, and first-century relevance. (Context before theology).
  2. Scripture Text (WEB): World English Bible text verbatim (unedited). Bolding allowed only for exact phrases defined in Annotations.
  3. Fulfillment Notes: Exactly 3–5 bullet points. STRICTLY 1 line per bullet—no mini-paragraphs.
  4. Annotations: Concise definitions of key terms, covenant symbols, or Hebrew idioms present in the passage.
  5. Fulfillment Commentary: 500+ WORDS MANDATORY. Uses numbered subheadings (e.g., 1) Title). Explains inauguration vs. consummation, what ended, and what began.
  6. Prophecy Hub Links: Exactly 3 internal tags pointing to Prophecy Hub themes.
  7. Cross-References: Exactly 3 curated scripture links (OT prophecy source, NT fulfillment, Epistle confirmation).
  8. New Creation Reality: Present application detailing believer identity, union, and freedom from condemnation today. No moralism or fear.
  9. Reflection: Exactly ONE presence-based question (no guilt or performance metrics).
  10. Footer, Share & Navigation Block: Mandatory UI containing share buttons, Ko-fi widget, and site navigation.

2) CORE THEOLOGICAL & EDITORIAL LOCKS

  • No Reader-Facing Meta-Language: NEVER explain interpretation methods on chapter pages. Banned: "from a preterist perspective," "we interpret," "in this lens," "this project reads." Assert meaning directly.
  • Word Floor Enforcement: The Fulfillment Commentary heading block must meet or exceed 500 words. Any page under 500 words is marked as a build FAIL.
  • Inauguration vs. Consummation: Earthly miracles and resuscitations (AD 30–33) are inaugural signs/types. The AD 70 Parousia is the consummation/antitype (Temple fell, Old Covenant ended, Hades emptied). Do not collapse the sign into the final event.
  • Demons / Unclean Spirits (Non-Dualistic Monotheism): In alignment with strict Hebrew monotheism (Isaiah 45:5–7; 1 Cor 8:4), reject Persian-style cosmic dualism. "Demons" and "unclean spirits" are NEVER immortal, independent supernatural evil gods. They are first-century Hebrew idioms, personifications, and cultural categories describing severe affliction, mental exile, pagan darkness, and Old Covenant curse-bondage under the law.
  • Exorcisms & Divine Accommodation: Frame exorcisms as Jesus using the active vocabulary and dramatic idioms of his first-century audience to demonstrate Kingdom authority—analogous to Him "rebuking" a fever (Luke 4:39) or "rebuking" the sea (Mark 4:39) without attributing literal personhood to wind or sickness. Expulsion of unclean spirits is the inaugural fulfillment of Zechariah 13:2, consummated in AD 70.
  • Satan / Devil: Defined as the covenantal Accuser/Adversary tied to Law-condemnation, carnal flesh, and apostate persecuting systems—NOT a supernatural fallen angel.
  • Rome $\neq$ Legion: Pagan nations (Rome) serve as God's OT "rod of anger" against apostate Jerusalem, not the demonic entity being cast out.
