The Fulfilled Prophecy Bible — Locked Standard

Non-Negotiable Format for Every Chapter Page

If a chapter page breaks this standard, it is a fail and must be corrected. This page and _AI_PROMPT.txt now lock the same order.

Project scope: WEB text verbatim, Fulfillment Notes, and Study Commentary. AD 30–70 covenant transition. New Creation as present reality.

1) Chapter Header (Required)

  • Book + Chapter title
  • One-line fulfilled theme (present reality, not speculation)

2) Scripture Text (WEB) — Primary Text

  • World English Bible, verbatim, unedited
  • Readable paragraph flow
  • Optional “Text source: WEB” line
Rule: The value-add is notes + Study Commentary, not rewritten Scripture.

3) Fulfillment Notes (Required — Brief)

Immediately after Scripture: 3–5 notes, one line each.

  • Audience relevance
  • Prophetic idiom
  • First-century resolution (AD 30–70)
Fail: missing notes, or notes that become paragraphs.

4) Immediate Context Block (Required)

One short paragraph: who speaks, who is addressed, what covenant moment, why it mattered then.

Rule: Context comes before theology.

5) Study Commentary (The Spine)

  • What the text meant in its original setting
  • How it resolves in the AD 30–70 transition
  • What ended (law / temple / priesthood / age / condemnation)
  • What replaced it (presence / Spirit / New Creation life)
Rule: No “already / not yet.” Say what ended. Say what began.
Length: 700+ words. Numbered subheadings. No plot recap of the WEB block. No method lecture on the page.

6) Prophecy Hub Links

Required when the chapter has judgment, coming/clouds, resurrection, heaven-and-earth, or kingdom-transfer language. Exactly 3 internal links.

Fail: prophetic tone with no Hub link.

7) Cross-References

  • 3–8 max
  • OT source + NT fulfillment + one epistle support

8) Key Idioms & Language Notes

Only when the phrase is in the chapter. Short definitions. No lexicon essays.

9) New Creation Reality (Today)

Identity, union, presence, freedom from condemnation, life in the Spirit. No moralism, fear, or end-times anxiety.

10) Reflection Prompt

Exactly one presence-based question. Not guilt. Not performance.

11) Navigation, Share, and Ko-fi

Top and bottom nav:

  • Bible Index | Book Index | Preface (if the book has one) | Previous | Next | Prophecy Hub

After the reflection, every chapter includes:

  • Share this study: X Twitter | Facebook | Email
  • Ko-fi button to https://ko-fi.com/B0B61QC1BU
Fail: missing nav, missing share, or missing Ko-fi.

Theological locks

  • The End = end of the Mosaic age (AD 70).
  • Coming / Parousia = covenantal vindication and judgment on Jerusalem.
  • Kingdom = present New Creation reality.
  • Resurrection in NT timing texts = covenantal transition into New Creation life.
  • Heaven and earth = covenant-world language.
  • AD 30–33 miracles, exorcisms, and resuscitations are inaugural signs and pledges. AD 70 is the consummation. Do not collapse them.
  • Rome is the rod against apostate Jerusalem. Legion is Satanic bondage and Old Covenant curse — not Rome.
  • Physical mortality continues. Covenantal death and condemnation of the old order ended.
Fail: futurism, already/not-yet, method language on the chapter page, or hedging in the commentary.

Pass / Fail Summary

A chapter FAILS if:

  • It sounds future-oriented without fulfilled clarification
  • Study Commentary is under 700 words or is a Scripture recap
  • It lacks Prophecy Hub linkage where prophetic language is present
  • It skips commentary, share, or Ko-fi
  • It breaks this section order

A chapter PASSES if:

  • Fulfillment is unmistakable
  • Context is clear
  • Language is clean and declarative
  • Structure and chrome match this page
