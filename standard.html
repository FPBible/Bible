1) LOCKED 10-PART CHAPTER PAGE SEQUENCE & DOM STRUCTURE

Every chapter page must render the following 10 sections in this exact order as flat siblings (no wrapping <section> tags):

  1. Immediate Context: Single direct paragraph stating speaker, original first-century audience, specific covenant moment, and immediate temporal context. (Context before theology).
  2. Scripture Text (WEB): World English Bible text verbatim (unedited). Bolding allowed only for exact phrases defined in Annotations.
  3. Fulfillment Notes: Exactly 3–5 bullet points. STRICTLY 1 line per bullet—no mini-paragraphs or wrapped multi-sentence lines.
  4. Annotations: Concise, rigorous definitions of key terms, covenant symbols, or Hebrew idioms present in the passage inside a styled block.
  5. Fulfillment Commentary: STRICT 500+ WORDS MANDATORY. Uses at least 4 numbered subheadings (e.g., <h3>1) Title</h3>). Explains inauguration (AD 30–33) vs. consummation (AD 70), Old Covenant dismantling, and New Covenant reality.
  6. Prophecy Hub Links: Exactly 3 internal tags pointing to Prophecy Hub themes.
  7. Cross-References: Exactly 3 curated scripture links (Old Testament prophecy source, New Testament fulfillment, Epistle confirmation).
  8. New Creation Reality: Present-day application detailing believer identity, union with Christ, absolute security, and complete freedom from Law-condemnation. Zero moralism, performance-driven fear, or legalism.
  9. Reflection: Exactly ONE presence-based question focused on resting in Christ's finished work (no guilt or self-effort metrics).
  10. Footer, Share & Navigation Block: Mandatory UI containing share buttons (𝕏, Facebook, Email), Ko-fi button, and bottom pill navigation.

  • Word Floor Enforcement: The Fulfillment Commentary heading block must meet or exceed 500 words (aim for 600 words). Any page with a commentary under 500 words is flagged as an automatic build FAIL.
  • Inauguration vs. Consummation: Earthly miracles and resuscitations (AD 30–33) are inaugural signs/types demonstrating Kingdom authority. The AD 70 Parousia is the consummation/antitype (Temple fell, Old Covenant ended, Hades emptied, Kingdom fully established). Never collapse the inaugural sign into the final event or treat miracles as ongoing Old Covenant signs.
  • Demons / Unclean Spirits (Non-Dualistic Monotheism): In strict alignment with ancient Hebrew monotheism (Isaiah 45:5–7; 1 Cor 8:4), reject Persian or pagan cosmic dualism. "Demons" and "unclean spirits" are NEVER immortal, independent supernatural evil entities or fallen angels. They are first-century Hebrew idioms, personifications, and cultural categories describing severe physical/mental affliction, mental exile, pagan darkness, and Old Covenant curse-bondage under the Law.
  • Exorcisms & Divine Accommodation: Frame exorcisms as Jesus using the active vocabulary and dramatic idioms of his first-century audience to demonstrate Kingdom authority over curse and exile—analogous to Him "rebuking" a fever (Luke 4:39) or "rebuking" the sea (Mark 4:39) without attributing literal personhood to wind or disease. Expulsion of unclean spirits is the inaugural fulfillment of Zechariah 13:2, consummated in AD 70.
  • Satan / Devil: Defined as the covenantal Accuser/Adversary (*satan*) tied to Law-condemnation, carnal flesh, and apostate persecuting systems (the Old Covenant religious establishment/Judean leadership)—NOT a cosmic fallen archangel.
  • Rome ≠ Legion / Demonic Entities: Pagan nations (Rome) serve as God's OT "rod of anger" (cf. Isa 10) against apostate Jerusalem, not a spiritual evil realm operating outside God's sovereign design.
  • Audience Relevance Priority: Evaluate all time-statements (*tachos*, *en tasei*, *at hand*, *this generation*) as bounded strictly to the first-century hearers.
